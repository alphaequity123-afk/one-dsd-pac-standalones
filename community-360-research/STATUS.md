**Checked:** October 31, 2026

## Where things stand

All 39 communities are finished, each with a profile, business list, corrections file, calendar entries and sources list, and the calendar covers all of them. The 10 communities first left thin by web search limits (karen, somali, african-american, latino, mexican, vietnamese, oromo, ethiopian, rural, indian) have had a second pass; what is still not established is listed below for every community. ## How dates are treated

- Commercial calendar sites do not confirm a date. They only add a note.
- Official sources that disagree confirm nothing. Holi 2027 stays unconfirmed because the Government of India's holiday list says March 23 and the panchang says March 22.
- Moon-sighted dates are never shown as confirmed.
- On October 31, 2026, Korean and Lebanese dates that rested only on commercial holiday sites (publicholidays.co.kr, calendarlabs.com) were changed to unconfirmed. Onam 2026 has since been set to unconfirmed. One date from Gary's computer still rests on such a source: the Hmong American Day Festival 2026 (a Scouting outreach calendar).
- The `.ics` file carries confirmed dates only.
