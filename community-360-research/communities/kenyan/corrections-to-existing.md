# Corrections to the existing Kenyan Minnesota page

Page: https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/kenyan
Checked: October 31, 2026. Each item gives the claim, what the opened source says, the link and the date checked.

## Errors or outdated claims that should change

1. **Claim (repeated in "Who this is not," "Counts, Dated," "Education," "Work, Markets," and elsewhere):** Compass (ACS 2019-2023) counts about 20,967 Kenyan Minnesotans, 77.3 percent in the Twin Cities and 22.7 percent in Greater Minnesota; 44.1 percent 17 or younger; 4.6 percent 65+; 66.6 percent foreign-born; 65.8 percent speak a language other than English; 24.4 percent speak English less than "very well"; 94.1 percent high school or higher; 38.9 percent bachelor's or higher; median household income about $77,518 (2022 dollars); poverty 12.6 percent; owners 37.1 percent, renters 62.9 percent; household size 3.50; disability 5.2 percent; working-age employment 89.9 percent.
   **Source says:** Compass now shows ACS 2020-2024: 20,222 (give or take 3,897); 71.4 percent Twin Cities and 28.6 percent Greater Minnesota (each give or take 7.9 points); 40.8 percent 17 or younger; 65+ suppressed (too few to publish); 68.8 percent foreign-born; 65.1 percent speak a language other than English; 26.5 percent speak English less than "very well"; 91.5 percent high school or higher; 39.4 percent bachelor's or higher; median household income $92,570 (2024 dollars, give or take $19,152); poverty 10.6 percent; owners 40.7 percent, renters 59.3 percent; household size 3.31; disability 5.8 percent; working-age employment 90.9 percent. All figures should be updated and dated 2020-2024, with margins of error, because several changes are within the margin.
   **Link:** https://www.mncompass.org/topics/demographics/cultural-communities/kenyan . Checked Oct. 31, 2026.

2. **Claim:** "Minnesota holds a real community, although it is not the U.S. capital of it."
   **Source says:** By the ACS birthplace count for 2020-2024, Minnesota has 16,726 Kenya-born residents, second among states only to Texas (22,911). LeadingAge Minnesota's workforce director told LeadingAge in 2024 that Minnesota's Kenyan population is the largest per capita of any state. The page should say Minnesota is the second-largest Kenya-born population in the U.S. and that the per-capita claim is one person's statement.
   **Links:** https://api.censusreporter.org/1.0/data/show/acs2024_5yr?table_ids=B05006&geo_ids=040|01000US ; https://leadingage.org/how-an-eye-opening-visit-to-kenya-led-to-new-connections-and-recruitment-of-nursing-staff/ . Checked Oct. 31, 2026.

3. **Claim (Huldah Momanyi-Hiltsley profile):** the profile goes from her 2023 association election loss to her November 2024 win.
   **Source says:** Not an error, but incomplete: the Legislative Reference Library notes she was an unsuccessful candidate for Senate District 38 in 2022, and CCX Media reports Susan Pha beat her in the August 2022 DFL primary with 62 percent. CCX also reports she won the August 2024 House primary over Wynfred Russell by 50 votes. The 64.78 percent general-election figure, the committees (Housing Finance and Policy, Human Services Finance and Policy, Legacy Finance) and the Nov. 3, 2026 ballot against Republican Charlie Kistler are all confirmed.
   **Links:** https://www.lrl.mn.gov/legdb/fulldetail?id=15634 ; https://ccxmedia.org/?s=kenyan ; https://theballotbrief.com/state/minnesota/hennepin-county/minnesota-house-district-38a . Checked Oct. 31, 2026.

4. **Claim (Tom Gitaa profile):** "He came to Minnesota in 1990 for college."
   **Source says:** MPR News (2022) says he moved from Kenya to Minnesota for college, planning to return, without a year. Metro State lists him as class of 1996. The 1990 year could not be confirmed from an opened page; the page's own Sahan Journal source could not be opened.
   **Links:** https://www.mprnews.org/episode/2022/06/07/minnesotas-longest-running-newspaper-serving-african-immigrants-looks-to-the-future ; https://www.metrostate.edu/buzz/spring-2025/news-and-notes . Checked Oct. 31, 2026.

5. **Claim:** Dates of Significance names Jamhuri, Madaraka and Mashujaa days but no dates, and no current Kenyan holiday list.
   **Source says:** Madaraka Day is June 1, Mashujaa Day October 20, Jamhuri Day December 12; Kenya's 2026 public holidays also include Mazingira Day on October 10 and Good Friday and Easter Monday. Adding the dates would help scheduling.
   **Links:** https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/42837-kenya-gazette-official-public-holidays-kenya ; https://www.calendarlabs.com/holidays/kenya/2026 . Checked Oct. 31, 2026.

- Mwanyagetinge draws many members from the Kisii (Abagusii) community and was founded in the mid-1980s, per Kenyans.co.ke, Feb. 20, 2023 (https://www.kenyans.co.ke/news/85894-mwanyagetinge-inside-largest-organised-group-kenyans-minnesota). - African Rhythms on KFAI, Sundays noon to 2 p.m., co-hosted by George "Jojo" Ndege; still airing in October 2026 (https://spinitron.com/KFAI/show/12952/African-Rhythms).
- LeadingAge Minnesota and related pipelines have named Kenya as a nursing source (https://www.leadingagemn.org/news-insights/exploring-global-workforce-solutions-in-nairobi-kenya/).
