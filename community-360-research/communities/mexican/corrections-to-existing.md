# Corrections to the existing Mexican Minnesota page

Page: https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/mexican
Checked: October 30, 2026

## Errors and outdated claims

**1. Compass figures are out of date and inconsistent.**
- Claim as stated: Population 229,576, with 38.0 percent 17 or younger, 4.0 percent 65 and up, 26.7 percent foreign-born, 58.3 percent speaking another language at home, 23.7 percent limited English, 71.5 percent high school or more, 18.9 percent bachelor's or more, median income about $66,000, poverty 12.8 percent, ownership 53.2 percent, household size 3.09, disability 8.4 percent, all labeled 2019 to 2023. Elsewhere the page gives 231,993 and labels that 2019 to 2023 as well.
- What the source says: Compass now reports 231,993, give or take 5,615, for 2020 to 2024, with 36.8 percent 17 or younger, 4.2 percent 65 and up, 27.5 percent foreign-born, 58.6 percent another language at home, 23.4 percent limited English, 73.4 percent high school or more, 20.6 percent bachelor's or more, median income $71,697, poverty 12.0 percent, ownership 53.1 percent, household size 2.99, disability 8.1 percent.
- Link: https://www.mncompass.org/topics/demographics/cultural-communities/mexican (checked Oct. 30, 2026)

**2. Twin Cities and Greater Minnesota split is out of date.**
- Claim as stated: 61.1 percent Twin Cities, 38.9 percent Greater Minnesota (Compass Points 2023).
- What the source says: 60.8 percent and 39.2 percent, each give or take 1.3 points, for 2020 to 2024.
- Link: https://www.mncompass.org/topics/demographics/cultural-communities/mexican (checked Oct. 30, 2026)

**3. The Cesar Chavez Street story has moved on, and the page does not say so.**
- Claim as stated: The street at the center of the parade "carries his name," and West Side Fiestas holds its parade on Cesar Chavez Street. No mention of renaming or of the end of Cesar Chavez Day.
- What the sources say: Minnesota repealed Cesar Chavez Day in 2026 and the governor proclaimed Farmworkers Day. The West Side Community Organization is running a renaming vote on Nov. 3, 2026, among five names. The street's name may change within weeks, and any address on the page using it (El Burrito Mercado, Boca Chica) may change too.
- Links: https://fox9.com/news/minnesota-recognizes-farmworkers-day-following-cesar-chavez-fallout-march-2026 ; https://www.wsco.org/streetrenaming (checked Oct. 30, 2026)

**4. A school name to watch.**
- Claim as stated: None found in what was read of the page. This is flagged in case the name Academia Cesar Chavez appears in any part of the page or its downloads.
- What the source says: The school's old web address now leads to Academia In Lak'ech at 1801 Lacrosse Ave., with a 2026 footer. MPR reported a St. Paul charter school removed Chávez's name. No statement from the school about the change was found.
- Links: https://www.academiainlakech.org/ ; https://www.mprnews.org/story/2026/07/28/cesar-chavez-street-st-paul-renaming-feedback-deadline (checked Oct. 30, 2026)

**5. "No Minnesota study" on disability is too strong as worded.**
- Claim as stated: The page found no Minnesota study of Mexican-heritage families and disability.
- What the sources say: That holds for Mexican-only work, but Minnesota evidence on Latino and Hispanic families exists and should be named: a 2014 Department of Health study with 17 Latino parents, 2021 reports for DHS on service use, and University of Minnesota autism tracking with 2022 data.
- Links: https://www.leg.mn.gov/docs/2014/mandated/140404.pdf ; https://developtoolmn.org/dhs/assets/racial-and-ethnic-comparison-report-quantatitive_tcm1053-663962.pdf ; https://ici.umn.edu/news/new-autism-prevalence-findings-in-minnesota (checked Oct. 30, 2026)

**6. Operation Metro Surge is not named, and the page's business picture is thin.**
- Claim as stated: Refers only to "a surge of federal agents."
- What the sources say: Reporting names the operation and dates its effects, including city loss estimates, the June 14, 2026 closing of Colonial Market on Lake Street, and Mercado Central's reported 85 percent January revenue loss.
- Links: https://sahanjournal.com/?p=83245 ; https://sahanjournal.com/?p=85331 ; https://tcbmag.com/tcb-show/the-story-of-mercado-central-and-25-years-in-the-heart-of-south-minneapolis/ (checked Oct. 30, 2026)

## Details to fix or soften

**7. Founding of GAP.**
- Claim as stated: Sister Mary Giovanni Gourhan founded the Guadalupe Alternative Program (now GAP) in 1967.
- What the source says: MNopedia names it the Guadalupe Area Plan, proposed in 1966 and operating by 1967.
- Link: https://www.mnhs.org/mnopedia/search/index/thing/chicano-movement-westside-st-paul (checked Oct. 30, 2026)

**8. Bracero dates.**
- Claim as stated: 1942 to 1964.
- What the sources say: MNopedia's Migrant Workers entry agrees. Its Minnesotanos timeline says 1943 to 1964. The internal difference is MNopedia's, so 1942 to 1964 can stay, but readers should know the two differ.
- Links: https://www.mnhs.org/mnopedia/search/index/group/migrant-workers ; https://www.mnhs.org/mnopedia/search/index/minnesotanos-latino-journeys-minnesota (checked Oct. 30, 2026)

**9. La Prensa de Minnesota as "first."**
- Claim as stated: MNopedia calls it the first Spanish-language newspaper in Minnesota.
- What the source says: Accurately reported, but MNopedia also uses narrower wording, first statewide Spanish-language and bilingual newspaper, and the Minnesota Historical Society holds another paper, *La Voz*, whose dates were not checked. Suggest the narrower wording.
- Links: https://www.mnhs.org/mnopedia/search/index/thing/la-prensa-de-minnesota ; https://libguides.mnhs.org/mexican (checked Oct. 30, 2026)

**10. Our Lady of Guadalupe merger with St. Michael.**
- Claim as stated: The parish merged with St. Michael in West St. Paul in 2019 and was named a shrine, citing The Catholic Spirit.
- What the source says: The Catholic Spirit's shrine article, as read, does not mention a merger. The shrine designation is supported. The merger could not be supported from what was opened.
- Link: https://thecatholicspirit.com/news/local-news/st-paul-parish-named-diocesan-shrine-to-our-lady-of-guadalupe/ (checked Oct. 30, 2026)

**11. Latino metro figure.**
- Claim as stated: About 180,000 Latino residents across the Twin Cities metro in 2020.
- What the sources say: Not checked against the state's figures here. The state reports about 381,000 Latino Minnesotans statewide now; the old metro number should carry its date plainly or be replaced.
- Link: https://mn.gov/mmb-stat/documents/budget/research-and-data/2026/summary-of-agencies-programs-activities/latino-affairs-council.pdf (checked Oct. 30, 2026)

**12. Jason Chavez's family origins.**
- Claim as stated: Parents from Puebla, Mexico.
- What the source says: The City of Minneapolis page opened does not state this. The page's other sources were not reopened, so this could not be supported here, though it was not contradicted.
- Link: https://www.minneapolismn.gov/government/city-council/members/ward-9/about-jason-chavez (checked Oct. 30, 2026)

**13. Gaps in history.** The page names sugar beets and packing only briefly. It lacks the 1930s repatriation, the destruction of the West Side Flats after the 1952 and 1957 floods, the 2005 opening of the consulate, Worthington's 2006 raid, the Austin plant illness, and the place-based ties of Axochiapan to Minneapolis and Maltrata to Northfield. See profile.md.
