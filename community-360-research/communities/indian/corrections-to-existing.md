# Corrections to the existing Indian Minnesota page

Page: https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/indian
Checked: October 30, 2026. Each item gives the claim, what the opened source says, the link and the date checked.

## Errors or claims that should change

1. **Claim:** About 51,909 people; 89.7 percent Twin Cities; 27.8 percent under 18; 3.9 percent 65 and older; 66.1 percent foreign-born; 80.3 percent bachelor's or more; median household income about $124,931; poverty 5.4 percent; 58.1 percent owner-occupied (ACS 2019 to 2023).
   **Source says:** Compass now reports 2020 to 2024: 51,618 (give or take 3,878); 90.0 percent Twin Cities; 26.6 percent under 18; 4.7 percent 65 and older; 64.4 percent foreign-born; 78.9 percent bachelor's or more; $133,600 median household income in 2024 dollars; 5.5 percent poverty; 61.8 percent owner-occupied.
   **Link:** https://www.mncompass.org/topics/demographics/cultural-communities/indian . Checked Oct. 30, 2026.

2. **Claim:** Sahan Journal "2025 festival roundup" for "up to 20,000 visitors," described as "Sahan, 2025."
   **Source says:** The article was posted Aug. 15, 2024, about IndiaFest on Aug. 17, 2024.
   **Link:** https://sahanjournal.com/arts-culture/indiafest-afrocentric-music-japanese-obon-festival-como-park/ . Checked Oct. 30, 2026.

3. **Claim:** 27.2 percent of "individuals" live alone.
   **Source says:** Could not support as stated. The state chartbook counts one-person households: 4,600 of 17,900 households, about 26 percent. That is a share of households, not of individuals. I did not open a Compass row stating the page's figure.
   **Link:** https://mn.gov/admin/assets/The%20Economic%20Status%20of%20Minnesotans%202026_tcm36-717691.pdf . Checked Oct. 30, 2026.

4. **Claim:** The temple's site says it is the largest Hindu temple in North America.
   **Source says:** Could not support. The temple's home and about pages I opened make no such claim. MPR News in 2009 says largest in Minnesota and one of the largest in the U.S.; CCX Media in 2021 says one of the largest in North America.
   **Links:** https://www.hindutemplemn.org/about ; https://www.mprnews.org/story/2009/06/26/minnesotas-largest-hindu-temple-gets-grand-ceremony ; https://ccxmedia.org/news/hindu-temple-in-maple-grove-celebrates-15th-anniversary . Checked Oct. 30, 2026.

5. **Claim:** India Association renamed in 1993; first India Day 1983; moved to Landmark Center 1985.
   **Source says:** Sources disagree and the page should show it. IAM says renamed 1993, first India Day in St. Paul 1983, Landmark Center 1985. MNopedia says renamed 1980 and first India Day at Landmark Center in 1983. Sahan Journal in 2024 says it started in 1985 at Landmark Center.
   **Links:** https://iamn.org/about/ ; https://www.mnhs.org/mnopedia/search/index/ganges-ten-thousand-lakes-immigration-india-minnesota ; https://sahanjournal.com/arts-culture/indiafest-afrocentric-music-japanese-obon-festival-como-park/ . Checked Oct. 30, 2026.

6. **Claim:** Aparna Ramaswamy "became Ragamala's co-artistic director in 2002" with no current title.
   **Source says:** True of 2002 per MNopedia. The company's page now lists her as Executive Artistic Director, Ranee as Co-Artistic Director, and Ashwini Ramaswamy as Choreographic Associate. MNopedia also notes David Whetstone left in 1996.
   **Links:** https://www.ragamaladance.org/about ; https://www.mnhs.org/mnopedia/search/index/group/ragamala-dance-company . Checked Oct. 30, 2026.

7. **Claim:** MNopedia poverty figure (under 3.4 percent) shown with the 2017 date.
   **Source says:** Dated, and MNopedia gives no year. Newer: Compass 5.5 percent (give or take 2.1); chartbook about 3,000 of 43,500 for 2019 to 2023. The page should not leave a reader thinking poverty is near zero.
   **Links:** https://www.mnhs.org/mnopedia/search/index/ganges-ten-thousand-lakes-immigration-india-minnesota ; https://www.mncompass.org/topics/demographics/cultural-communities/indian . Checked Oct. 30, 2026.

8. **Claim:** Bachelor's figure of 80.3 percent shown as the group's measure, with no other measure.
   **Source says:** The chartbook gives 84 percent for ages 25 to 64, and says it is the highest share of any cultural group. Compass gives 78.9 percent for 25 and older. Both are correct for different ages.
   **Link:** https://mn.gov/admin/assets/The%20Economic%20Status%20of%20Minnesotans%202026_tcm36-717691.pdf . Checked Oct. 30, 2026.

9. **Claim:** Ashwin Madia appears among the community's people.
   **Source says:** His 2008 race details are correct, but neither the election archive nor MNopedia states his family background, and I could not open a page that does. The new profile leaves him out until a source is found.
   **Links:** https://mn.electionarchives.lib.umn.edu/candidate/ashwin-madia/ ; https://www.mnhs.org/mnopedia/search/index/ganges-ten-thousand-lakes-immigration-india-minnesota . Checked Oct. 30, 2026.
