# Corrections to the existing Ethiopian Minnesota page

Page: https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/ethiopian

## Outdated figures that should change

1. **Claim:** Population about 40,324 (Compass, ACS 2019-2023).
   **Source says:** Compass now gives a 2024 total of 53,754, give or take 12,826, and uses a pooled 2020-2024 base of 45,159, give or take 5,404, for its other figures. Compass's own hub page shows 45,159 but labels the source 2019-2023, so the two Compass pages disagree on the label.
   **Links:** https://www.mncompass.org/topics/demographics/cultural-communities/ethiopian ; https://www.mncompass.org/topics/demographics/cultural-communities .
2. **Claim:** The rest of the Compass block: 89.4 percent Twin Cities; 43.5 percent age 17 or younger; 3.6 percent 65 or older; 61.7 percent foreign-born; 74.9 percent other language; 30.4 percent limited English; 81.5 percent high school or higher; 24.6 percent bachelor's or higher; median income about $57,106; poverty 13.0 percent; 34.7 percent owners; household size 3.09; disability 5.4 percent.
   **Source says (2020-2024, in 2024 dollars):** 89.7 percent Twin Cities; 43.8 percent age 17 or younger; 3.5 percent 65 or older; 59.8 percent foreign-born; 76.2 percent other language; 30.2 percent limited English; 79.5 percent high school or higher (100 minus 20.5 percent without a diploma); 24.1 percent bachelor's or higher; median income $63,667; poverty 10.9 percent; 35.6 percent owners; household size 3.30; disability 5.5 percent.
   **Link:** https://www.mncompass.org/topics/demographics/cultural-communities/ethiopian .
## Omissions a reader would notice

3. **Claim:** Umbrella framing lists "Oromo, Amhara, Tigrayan, Gurage, Sidama, and other peoples."
   **Source says:** Anuak Minnesotans are a distinct community with a Minnesota history and a 2008 estimate of 2,500 to 3,000 (Akway Cham, head of the Anywaa Community Association in North America), the largest group thought to live outside Africa. The page profiles Oballa Oballa of Gambella without naming the Anuak community at all. No opened source states his own background in those terms, so this concerns the community, not him.
   **Links:** https://www.mprnews.org/story/2008/05/30/anuak ; https://www.grandforksherald.com/newsmd/anuak-community-in-worthington-pleads-for-peace-in-ethiopian-homeland .
4. **Claim:** "Debre Selam Medhanealem Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church" named as a place with no detail.
   **Source says:** It opened a new building at 2601 30th Ave. S. in Seward, with an open house on April 18, 2026, at a cost of nearly $11 million including land. This is one of the largest recent events in the community's life. Its old home at 4401 Minnehaha Ave. dates from 1999.
   **Link:** https://www.longfellownokomismessenger.com/stories/debre-selam-medhanealem-ethiopian-orthodox-tewahedo-churchfinds-new-home-in-seward,161305 .
5. **Claim:** Historical events list "2020-2022: War in Tigray, still shaping some households."
   **Source says:** Fighting has resumed. Human Rights Watch reported that fighting erupted on Sept. 22, 2026 in Tigray, Amhara and Afar, after clashes and airstrikes since August. Al Jazeera reported on Oct. 15, 2026 that Ethiopian and allied forces had entered Aksum and that the TPLF had withdrawn from Mekelle, with Ethiopia and Eritrea accusing each other. The past-tense framing is out of date.
   **Links:** https://www.hrw.org/news/2026/09/24/ethiopia-renewed-tigray-fighting-shows-need-to-protect-civilians ; https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/15/ethiopian-forces-expand-control-in-tigray-as-militia-claims-eritrean-losses ; https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/9/ethiopia-and-eritrea-accuse-one-another-of-launching-war .
6. **Claim:** Services note mentions "deportation climate" with no specifics.
   **Source says:** Temporary Protected Status for Ethiopia was terminated by notice of Dec. 15, 2025, a court stay was lifted, and Fragomen reports benefits ended Aug. 18, 2026 while a constitutional challenge continues.
   **Links:** https://www.fragomen.com/insights/united-states-federal-district-court-allows-termination-of-tps-for-ethiopia-to-proceed.html ; https://www.bal.com/immigration-news/united-states-termination-of-ethiopia-tps-designation-effective-feb-13-2026/ .
7. **Claim:** Ethiopian Sports Federation tournament "held in the Twin Cities" in 2026, and Ethiopian New Year mentioned only through a 2022 participant.
   **Source says:** Most games were at TCO Stadium, Eagan, some at Saint Thomas Academy, June 27 to July 4, 2026. The Ethiopian Community in Minnesota invited people to a New Year celebration at Highland Park Pavilion on Sept. 13, 2026, so the page's "no later edition found" framing for public New Year events should not be read as no public events.
   **Links:** https://esfna.org/tournament/ ; https://mefthe.com/events/celebrate-ethiopian-new-year-in-minnesota/ .
## Conflicts between sources

8. **Claim:** ZeHabesha "founded 2008 by Henok A. Degfu."
   **Source says:** Sahan Journal (2020) says 2008. ZeHabesha's own page now says it was founded in 2005 and does not name a founder in the portion read. Report both.
   **Links:** https://sahanjournal.com/news/ethiopian-airlines-africas-largest-carrier-sues-minnesota-journalist-and-his-independent-newspaper/ ; https://zehabesha.com/ .
9. **Claim:** Cedar-Riverside is "primarily a Somali hub, not a default Ethiopian one."
   **Source says:** The International Institute of Minnesota's guide (last updated January 2017) names Seward and Cedar-Riverside as the Minneapolis neighborhoods with the largest Ethiopian populations, and The Red Sea and Dilla's are both there. The page's caution may be fair for services, but as stated it could mislead. No current neighborhood count was found to settle it.
   **Links:** https://iimn.org/publication/finding-common-ground/ ; https://mplswithlove.com/guides/best-ethiopian-east-african-food .
10. **Claim:** Population estimates listed include ECM's 45,000-50,000 and Mpls.St.Paul's "at least 30,000" (2021).
    **Source says:** Fair as far as they go. Add Mpls.St.Paul 2024 (nearly 40,000 in the metro), Chala 2026 (35,000 to 50,000), and the AP/MPR 2019 figure of about 100,000, which is an outlier without a source and should not be repeated without that warning. The ECM document is undated in its text; the 2022 date on the page may come from the bill and could not be separately confirmed.
    **Links:** https://mspmag.com/eat-and-drink/the-new-ethiopian-scene/ ; https://www.minnpost.com/community-voices/2026/06/celebrate-ethiopians-and-resilience-at-twin-cities-soccer-tourney-ethiopian-sport-federation/ ; https://www.mprnews.org/story/2019/04/27/ethiopia-consulate-stpaul ; https://www.house.mn.gov/comm/docs/herYhSCSj0izw9flFc5c4g.pdf .
11. **Claim:** Timkat listed by name with no Minnesota context; page gives Jan. 19 implicitly.
    **Source says:** One calendar guide says Timkat is always Jan. 19, while the calendar seed computes Jan. 20 for 2028. Unresolved; see calendar-entries.json.
    **Link:** https://weblogtheworld.com/countries/africa-africa/timkat-ethiopia-epiphany-sacred-water-festival .
## Claims checked and found supported

- ECM at 616 Rice St. with Monday and Friday hours; eight-by-ten-foot office; $2.5 million ask at 1630 University Ave. in HF 3908 (the ask appears in the House document's own summary): supported. https://ethiopiancommunitymn.org/contact/ ; https://www.house.mn.gov/comm/docs/herYhSCSj0izw9flFc5c4g.pdf
- Selassie Cathedral, formed 2014, consecrated 2015, at 2601 Minnehaha Ave.: supported. https://minnesotaselassie.org/index.php/clergy/brief-history
- Debre Bisrat at Rosemount, about 200 members, July 2026 fire about three months after moving in: supported. https://www.fox9.com/news/community-rallies-around-rosemount-ethiopian-orthodox-church-after-fire ; https://www.gofundme.com/f/support-debre-bisrat-minnesota-to-rebuild
- Gov. Walz declaring July 3 Ethiopian Day; federation nearly not holding the tournament in Minnesota because of enforcement: supported. https://www.mprnews.org/story/2026/06/30/ethiopian-soccer-tournament-draws-thousands-despite-immigration-fears
- Consulate opened in St. Paul in 2019: supported. https://www.mprnews.org/story/2019/04/27/ethiopia-consulate-stpaul
- Oballa's appointment 5-1 to Ward 3 after a Ward 1 term and a 2022 at-large loss: supported. https://www.kaaltv.com/?p=875617
- Kamal Mohamed's Parcelle with planned North Loop and Wayzata sites: Parcelle's own site marks both "Coming Soon" and also lists a LaSalle Plaza location downtown. https://parcelleorganics.com
