# Corrections to the existing Hmong Minnesota page

Page: https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/hmong
Checked: October 30, 2026. Each item gives the claim, what the opened source says, the link and the date checked.

## Errors or claims that should change

1. **Claim:** "Later Minnesota ADDM-style work reported lower prevalence among Hmong children."
   **Source says:** True for the combined 2014 to 2016 data (ratio 0.6 to white children). But the 2022 data show 1 in 28 (3.62 percent) of Hmong 8-year-olds identified, the same as 1 in 28 overall in that study, with 54 percent of Hmong children with IQ tests on file having intellectual disability, against 37 percent overall. The page gives the older picture as if current.
   **Links:** https://addm.umn.edu/detailed-findings/press-briefing-2025-09-26 ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41661527/ . Checked Oct. 30, 2026.

2. **Claim:** May 14 is "Hmong American Day" or "Hmong American / Hmong-Lao veterans memory," with no legal status given.
   **Source says:** Minnesota law names May 14 Hmong Special Guerrilla Units Remembrance Day (first enacted 2019, amended 2023). The law says the governor issues a proclamation, flags fly at half-staff on Capitol grounds, schools are encouraged to teach Hmong history, businesses may close, and "an employee may request the day off." St. Paul Public Schools separately uses Hmong American Day and says the state recognized that name in 2013.
   **Links:** https://www.revisor.mn.gov/statutes/cite/10.5805 ; https://www.spps.org/families/family-engagement-community-partnerships/parent-advisory-councils/hmong-parent-advisory-council/hmong-american-day . Checked Oct. 30, 2026.

3. **Claim:** Hmong New Year in St. Paul "held since 1977."
   **Source says:** Not supported in anything opened. RiverCentre calls 2025 the 45th. The organizer's March 2025 testimony says "the last 44 years." MPR in 2023 said 43 years in St. Paul. These point to about 1980 or 1981, not 1977.
   **Links:** https://www.rivercentre.org/news/detail/minnesota-hmong-new-year-returns-to-saint-paul-rivercentre-november-29-30-2025 ; https://www.lrl.mn.gov/archive/minutes/senate/2025/envlegacy/20250325/envlegacy_20250325_SF2590-Maycee-Vang-testimony.pdf ; https://www.mprnews.org/episode/2023/11/20/st-paul-hmong-new-year-celebration-preserves-hmong-culture . Checked Oct. 30, 2026.

4. **Claim:** First family, Dang Her and Shoua Moua, arrived "in December 1975."
   **Source says:** Sources disagree. MNopedia says November 5, 1975, and CBS follows it. Ramsey County History says December 1975. Should be given with both dates. Both sources also separate this family from Leng Wong's, the first refugees resettled here, in early 1976.
   **Links:** https://www.mnhs.org/mnopedia/search/index/hmong-and-hmong-americans-minnesota ; https://rchs.com/publishing/catalog/hmong-lives-in-ramsey-county/ ; https://www.cbsnews.com/minnesota/news/leng-wong-first-minnesota-hmong-refugee-vietnam-war/ . Checked Oct. 30, 2026.

5. **Claim:** Choua Lee elected to the St. Paul School Board in "November 1991."
   **Source says:** MNopedia says 1992. Report both; the existing page's own sources were not reopened.
   **Link:** https://www.mnhs.org/mnopedia/search/index/hmong-and-hmong-americans-minnesota . Checked Oct. 30, 2026.

6. **Claim:** Hmong Village "opened 2010."
   **Source says:** Racket says it has operated since 2010; MNopedia says 2009. Report both.
   **Links:** https://racketmn.com/how-hmong-village-changed-what-it-meant-to-be-hmong-in-st-paul ; https://www.mnhs.org/mnopedia/search/index/hmong-and-hmong-americans-minnesota . Checked Oct. 30, 2026.

## Outdated

7. **Claim:** All Compass figures, labeled 2019 to 2023 (about 99,912; 94.4 percent Twin Cities; 38.3 percent age 17 or younger; 33.4 percent foreign-born; 77.3 percent language other than English; 80.3 percent high school; 26.4 percent bachelor's; about $90,000; 9.1 percent poverty; 63.2 percent owners; household size 4.40).
   **Source says:** Compass now reports 2020 to 2024: 102,491 (plus or minus 5,546); 93.8 percent; 38.0 percent; 31.5 percent; 75.5 percent; 82.6 percent; 28.8 percent; $92,644 in 2024 dollars; 10.0 percent; 62.1 percent; 4.27. English less than very well is unchanged at 31.1 percent. Compass also has a disability row (8.4 percent, 6.4 percent under 65) the page does not use.
   **Link:** https://www.mncompass.org/topics/demographics/cultural-communities/hmong . Checked Oct. 30, 2026.
