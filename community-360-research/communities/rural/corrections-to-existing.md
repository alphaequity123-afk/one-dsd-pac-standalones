# Corrections to the existing Rural and Greater Minnesota page

Page: https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/rural

## Errors or claims that should change

1. **Claim:** "about 95 rural hospitals, with about 20 at risk of closing or major service cuts (MinnPost, April 2026)," presented as a reported figure.
   **Source says:** The April 16, 2026 MinnPost item is an opinion column by Barb Stinnett that gives no source for the figure. The 95 is supported by MDH, for its own definition. Another column (Aaron Wittnebel, May 20, 2026) says 23, nine by year's end. The Center for Healthcare Quality and Payment Reform found at least nine at immediate risk, reported in 2025. MDH counts 34 rural hospitals as financially distressed, which is a different measure. A farm magazine reports that Minnesota is not among the nine states it lists as most at risk. The page should show the range and name the column as opinion.
   **Links:** http://www.minnpost.com/community-voices/2026/04/rural-health-care-in-minnesota-is-at-risk-of-becoming-a-medical-wasteland/ ; http://www.minnpost.com/community-voices/2026/05/minnesotas-legislative-session-left-rural-healthcare-systems-exposed/ ; https://www.health.mn.gov/facilities/ruralhealth/ruraltrans/docs/rhtpnarrative.pdf ; https://www.kaxe.org/local-news/2025-07-11/seismic-medicaid-changes-will-be-rough-on-rural-hospitals ; https://www.dtnpf.com/agriculture/web/ag/magazine/article/2026/04/01/code-blue .
2. **Claim:** "Somali Minnesotans: about 21.9 percent in Greater Minnesota" (ACS 2019 to 2023); also rounded to "about 22 percent."
   **Source says:** Compass now gives 22.7 percent, give or take 3.6 points, for 2020 to 2024. The margin should be shown. The figures for Mexican Minnesotans remain near the page's number, now 39.2 percent, give or take 1.3.
   **Links:** https://www.mncompass.org/topics/demographics/cultural-communities/somali ; https://www.mncompass.org/topics/demographics/cultural-communities/mexican .
3. **Claim:** "about 40 percent of residents in isolated rural areas reported a chronic condition" (Compass/MDH, 2020).
   **Source says:** The original could not be found. MDH's newer document says roughly 51 percent of rural adults have at least one chronic condition, and 64 percent counting obesity, using its own wider rural definition and list of conditions. The two are not directly comparable, and the newer figure should appear.
   **Link:** https://www.health.mn.gov/facilities/ruralhealth/ruraltrans/docs/rhtpnarrative.pdf .
4. **Claim:** "about 45 percent of rural Minnesota hospitals no longer offer maternity care (Axios, January 2024)," also described as losing "labor and delivery."
   **Source says:** The 45 percent appears as stated in a January 31, 2024 repost of Axios. It describes hospitals without the service, not necessarily hospitals that lost it, so "no longer" should stay with the source's words. Newer figures exist: 50 rural hospitals without labor and delivery in 2026 and 8 closures since 2020 (Center for Healthcare Quality and Payment Reform, via Pennsylvania Office of Rural Health), and 18 counties with reduced or eliminated services from 2013 to 2023 (MDH).
   **Links:** https://www.ruralmn.org/axios-nearly-half-of-minnesotas-rural-hospitals-dont-offer-labor-and-delivery/ ; https://www.porh.psu.edu/124-rural-hospital-maternity-services-closed-closing-in-6-years-analysis/ ; https://www.health.mn.gov/facilities/ruralhealth/ruraltrans/docs/rhtpnarrative.pdf .
5. **Omission:** The page gives no definition of rural.
   **Source says:** MDH treats commuting codes 4 to 10 as rural, or 29.7 percent of Minnesotans. CRPD and the state demographer use four county groups, and Greater Minnesota usually means 80 counties. The numbers on the page cannot be read correctly without this.
   **Links:** https://www.health.mn.gov/facilities/ruralhealth/ruraltrans/docs/rhtpnarrative.pdf ; https://www.ruralmn.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/State-of-Rural-2026.pdf .
6. **Claim:** Centro Campesino "founded 1998; closed or dormant."
   **Source says:** MPR in July 2011 says its leader had served for more than a decade but gives no founding year. Neither the 1998 date nor the current status could be supported.
   **Link:** https://www.mprnews.org/story/2011/07/15/victor-contreras-centro-campesino-steps-down .
7. **Claim:** Farm and Rural Helpline materials "in English, Hmong, Somali, and Spanish."
   **Source says:** The helpline numbers are right. The department page opened does not list languages, so this could not be supported from it.
   **Link:** https://www.mda.state.mn.us/about/mnfarmerstress .
