# Corrections to the existing Lebanese Minnesota page

Page: https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/lebanese
Checked: October 31, 2026. Each item gives the claim, what the opened source says, the link and the date checked.

## Errors or claims that should change

1. **Claim:** Under St. Maron Maronite Catholic Church, the page cites "https://www.stmaron.org/history" as a source for the Minneapolis parish's history.
   **Source says:** stmaron.org is the site of the Eparchy of Saint Maron of Brooklyn, a different Maronite diocese. Minneapolis's St. Maron belongs to the Eparchy of Our Lady of Lebanon (GCatholic). The parish's own site is stmaron.com, which on Oct. 31, 2026, showed only a "New Site" placeholder. The link should be removed or replaced.
   **Links:** https://www.stmaron.org/history ; https://gcatholic.org/churches/usa-08/17077.htm ; https://www.stmaron.com/ . Checked Oct. 31, 2026.

2. **Claim:** Chorbishop Sharbel Maroun is described only as St. Maron's pastor (for example under "Institutions and history").
   **Source says:** On Aug. 28, 2026, the Vatican announced that Pope Leo XIV approved his election as Maronite archbishop of Damascus, Syria. The Archdiocese of Saint Paul and Minneapolis said he has served St. Maron for more than 35 years and "will be deeply missed." The page should add this and date it.
   **Links:** https://press.vatican.va/content/salastampa/en/bollettino/pubblico/2026/08/28/260828c.html ; https://www.archspm.org/statement-regarding-bishop-elect-sharbel-maroun/ . Checked Oct. 31, 2026.

3. **Claim:** Herb Mocol was mayor "1974–1986."
   **Source says:** His 2005 Mankato Free Press obituary says he was elected mayor in 1974 and held the job 12 years, which supports the page. The Blue Earth County Historical Society says 1975 to 1986. Report both. The obituary also says he served four years on the council; the page's "City Council president 1970–1974" detail was not found in the opened obituary text.
   **Links:** https://www.mankatofreepress.com/news/local_news/mankato-politician-mocol-dies/article_008fce39-aac5-5bfd-ab9e-730f2fe629e4.html ; https://blueearthcountyhistory.com/2021/08/04/lebanese-immigration-to-mankato/ . Checked Oct. 31, 2026.

4. **Claim:** St. George Antiochian Orthodox Church has been "in West St. Paul since 1973."
   **Source says:** The parish says it bought the West St. Paul land in 1968, built its multipurpose hall during 1973, and held its first service there in February 1974. "Since 1974" (first service) or "building since 1973" would be more exact.
   **Link:** https://www.mideastfest.com/who-we-are/ . Checked Oct. 31, 2026.

5. **Claim:** "There is no Cedar-Riverside of Lebanese Minnesota. Do not assume Minneapolis."
   **Source says:** No single dense district exists today, but the sources describe a historic and continuing center in Northeast Minneapolis around St. Maron, plus St. Paul's West Side (now West St. Paul and Mendota Heights) and Mankato. Compass puts 76.7 percent in the Twin Cities region. The page could name these places rather than only saying what the community is not.
   **Links:** https://www.mynortheaster.com/news/lebanese-came-to-northeast-in-waves/ ; https://www.mncompass.org/topics/demographics/cultural-communities/lebanese . Checked Oct. 31, 2026.

## Outdated

6. **Claim:** All Compass figures labeled ACS 2019-2023: about 6,959 people; 81.0 percent Twin Cities and 19.0 percent Greater Minnesota; 20.3 percent 17 or younger; 16.4 percent 65 or older; 8.5 percent foreign-born; 11.8 percent language other than English; 97.9 percent high school or higher; 50.5 percent bachelor's or higher; poverty 12.0 percent; median household income about $73,000; 74.5 percent owner-occupied; household size 2.14.
   **Source says:** Compass now reports 2020-2024: 7,232 people in the analysis (plus or minus 1,225) and a 2024 single-year total of 10,066 (plus or minus 3,576), which Compass calls its most accurate size estimate; 76.7 / 23.3 percent; 24.2 percent; 17.0 percent; 9.2 percent; 13.9 percent; 97.8 percent; 50.9 percent; poverty 8.8 percent (plus or minus 5.4); median $72,873 in 2024 dollars (plus or minus $21,005); 68.6 percent owners; 2.12. English less than "very well" remains suppressed. Compass also now shows a disability rate (13.0 percent, 10.5 percent under 65) that the page does not use. Note that the page's "2022 dollars" label for income no longer applies.
   **Link:** https://www.mncompass.org/topics/demographics/cultural-communities/lebanese . Checked Oct. 31, 2026.

7. **Claim:** Najwa Massad is "seeking a third term"; "The general election is on November 3, 2026."
   **Source says:** Confirmed as of the check date: KEYC (May 22, 2026) and the Mankato Free Press (Aug. 7, 2026) describe her campaign, and Radio Mankato's Aug. 12, 2026 results match the page's primary figures (2,378 votes, 41.79 percent; Hamann 2,267, 39.83 percent). The page will need updating after the election.
   **Links:** https://www.keyc.com/2026/05/22/candidates-step-forward-mankatos-2026-mayoral-election/ ; https://radiomankato.com/?p=437082 . Checked Oct. 31, 2026.

8. **Claim:** The disability section says nothing about Minnesota numbers beyond "no Minnesota study."
   **Source says:** Compass 2020-2024 reports 13.0 percent of Lebanese Minnesotans with a disability (plus or minus 4.3), 10.5 percent under 65 (plus or minus 4.6). Statewide, Compass says at least 12 percent. Wide margins; the group is older than many others.
   **Links:** https://www.mncompass.org/topics/demographics/cultural-communities/lebanese ; https://www.mncompass.org/topics/demographics/disability . Checked Oct. 31, 2026.

9. **Claim:** The page does not mention current immigration status questions for recent arrivals except in general terms.
   **Source says:** Lebanon's Temporary Protected Status was automatically extended through Nov. 27, 2026, after DHS missed its decision deadline (Federal Register, May 29, 2026); AP put the number of Lebanese covered nationwide at about 11,000. No Minnesota count was found.
   **Links:** https://public-inspection.federalregister.gov/2026-10704.pdf ; https://wtop.com/national/2026/05/trump-administration-grants-rare-tps-reprieve-extending-protections-for-11000-lebanese/ . Checked Oct. 31, 2026.

## Could not be supported from opened sources

10. **Claim:** "Christian and Muslim Lebanese both live here: Maronite, Orthodox, Catholic, Protestant; Sunni and Shiʿa."
    **Finding:** Maronite and Antiochian Orthodox parishes are well documented. No opened Minnesota source documents Lebanese Sunni or Shia Muslims, a Lebanese mosque, or Lebanese Protestant congregations in Minnesota. MPR (2006) described many Lebanese Americans in Minnesota as Christian. This is a gap in the public record; the page's wording should say the Muslim presence is not documented rather than state it.
    **Link:** https://www.mprnews.org/story/2006/07/20/isrealreax . Checked Oct. 31, 2026.

11. **Claim:** Sahan Journal, citing the 2022 ACS, reported about 8,000 Minnesotans of Lebanese ancestry, about 800 born in Lebanon.
    **Finding:** The Sahan Journal article could not be opened (repeated "Too Many Requests" responses), so these figures were not checked. Compass's newer 2024 single-year figure is 10,066.
    **Source:** Sahan Journal, "Lebanese Minnesotans sad, angry as Israel attacks homeland" (Oct. 22, 2024), as named on the existing page; it could not be opened on Oct. 31, 2026.

12. **Claim:** "Distrust of government has sources... 9/11-era surveillance, Gaza-era harassment, and ordinary ICE for some later arrivals," and statements about LGBTQ Lebanese Minnesotans' family climates.
    **Finding:** No opened Minnesota source about Lebanese Minnesotans supports these as stated. They may be reasonable cautions, but they are not evidenced for this community in anything found.

13. **Claim:** Zakia Deli was "Opened in 2007 by David Maalouf with his mother, Zakia."
    **Finding:** Consistent. Racket (March 19, 2025) says David Maalouf opened it in 2007 and named it after his mother; the Northeaster (2021) says Zakie Maalouf owns it and opened it with her son David. The spelling of her name differs between sources (Zakia, Zakie).
    **Links:** https://racketmn.com/best-budget-bites-9-49-grilled-chicken-sandwich-from-zakia-deli ; https://www.mynortheaster.com/news/lebanese-came-to-northeast-in-waves/ . Checked Oct. 31, 2026.
