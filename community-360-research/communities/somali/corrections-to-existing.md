# Corrections to the existing Somali Minnesota page

Page: https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/somali

## Errors or claims that should change

1. **Claim:** Population data use ACS 2019 to 2023: about 79,449 people; 78.1 percent Twin Cities; 47.1 percent 17 or younger; 3.8 percent 65 or older; 55.8 percent foreign-born; 85.3 percent other language; 36.6 percent English less than "very well"; median household income about $43,600; 36.4 percent below poverty; 86.6 percent rent; household size about 3.98.
   **Source says:** Compass now shows the 2020 to 2024 pool: 85,533 (give or take 7,711), with a separate 2024 total of 111,722 (give or take 20,401) that it calls the most accurate size estimate. Also 77.3 percent Twin Cities; 45.7 percent 17 or younger; 3.4 percent 65 or older; 52.2 percent foreign-born; 82.7 percent other language; 33.6 percent English less than "very well"; median $49,554; 30.9 percent below poverty; 84.4 percent rent; household size 4.07.
   **Link:** https://www.mncompass.org/topics/demographics/cultural-communities/somali .
2. **Claim:** Maangaar Summit is dated September 9, 2024.
   **Source says:** The summit was held September 2, 2024. September 9 is the article's publication date.
   **Link:** https://sahanjournal.com/immigration/maangaar-summit-somali-autism-minnesota/ .
3. **Claim:** Omar's biography stops at the August 2026 primary and November ballot.
   **Source says:** MPR News reported on Oct. 21 and 22, 2026 that federal agents had searched her Washington home the week before, in what the Justice Department calls a criminal investigation. The Homeland Security Secretary has referred to possible fraud in her family's immigration paperwork. Neither report says she has been charged, and she says she did nothing wrong. The page should carry this, dated and attributed, or say clearly when its biography stops.
   **Links:** https://www.mprnews.org/story/2026/10/22/rep-ilhan-omar-says-she-will-move-out-of-her-washington-dc-home-after-federal-search ; https://www.mprnews.org/story/2026/10/21/ilhan-omar-says-she-wont-be-intimidated-federal-investigation .
4. **Claim:** Omar's section cites Ballotpedia for the primary result.
   **Source says:** Ballotpedia is a wiki and should not be cited. MPR News confirms she is on the Nov. 3 ballot; an opened replacement for the Aug. 11 primary result was not found.
   **Link:** https://www.mprnews.org/story/2026/10/22/rep-ilhan-omar-says-she-will-move-out-of-her-washington-dc-home-after-federal-search .
5. **Claim:** Somali Community Resettlement Services is at 2115 Stevens Ave., "also Rochester and Faribault."
   **Source says:** Its own site says it previously had offices in those two cities and does not say they remain open. The site shows a 2021 copyright and contains unrelated spam links, so its current status needs a phone check.
   **Link:** https://somalcrs.org/ .
6. **Claim:** MPR News "counted" more than 80,000 Somali Americans in 2026.
   **Source says:** This is a host's spoken line in a radio introduction, not a count. It should not be used as a population figure.
   **Link:** https://www.mprnews.org/episode/2026/09/30/somali-brief-digital-outlet-focuses-on-somali-americans .
7. **Claim:** Dar Al-Hijrah "closed for a time" after January 2014 water damage from a fire next door.
   **Source says:** The January 1, 2014 fire killed three people living above a neighboring store. The mosque closed for more than a year and reopened in spring 2015.
   **Link:** https://digitours.augsburg.edu/items/show/9 .
8. **Claim:** Karmel Mall has "more than a hundred small businesses."
   **Source says:** AP and MNopedia say more than 100, but the owner told Sahan Journal 650 in June 2026 and KALW/MPR says hundreds. The page should give the range and who says what. A second site is also listed at 200 W. Lake St.
   **Links:** https://sahanjournal.com/business-work/minnesota-somali-businesses-struggle-after-immigration-crackdown/ ; https://www.kalw.org/2026-01-03/minnesota-somali-community-bears-brunt-of-trump-administration-policies ; https://www.visitlakestreet.com/ramadan-guide .
9. **Claim:** Somali American Social Service Association; Omar Nur as founder and director.
   **Source says:** KTTC spells it Somali America Social Services Association and Sahan Journal uses Somali American Social Service Association. Confirm the legal name.
   **Links:** https://www.kttc.com/2025/02/23/olmsted-county-immigrant-support-organization-provides-important-information/ ; https://sahanjournal.com/health/somali-minnesotans-challenges-diabetes/ .
10. **Claim:** Omar "arrived in the U.S. in 1995," born 1982, after about four years in a camp.
    **Source says:** Sahan Journal's Oct. 15, 2026 article says she was 8 when she arrived in the U.S., after four years in a camp, which does not fit with the 1982 birth year and 1995 arrival. The page's version looks more consistent; the two sources disagree. The same article dates her family's move to Minnesota to 1997.
    **Link:** https://sahanjournal.com/immigration/somali-community-minnesota-trump-omar-gang-activity/ .
## Things the page leaves out that a reader needs

- The fraud cases, stated carefully: 78 or 79 charged in Feeding Our Future, 66 convicted by May 21, 2026, two acquitted, the ringleader white and sentenced to 500 months, and further charges that remain allegations. Links: https://sahanjournal.com/public-safety/aimee-bock-feeding-our-future-sentencing-fraud-2/ ; https://mprnews.org/story/2026/10/30/fraud-announcement-in-st-cloud-to-have-scott-bessent-kash-patel
- The end of Temporary Protected Status for Somalia, allowed by a federal judge in August 2026 and on appeal. Link: https://mprnews.org/story/2026/08/17/judges-order-allows-end-tps-protections-people-somalia
- The state's March 2026 study of 26,344 Somali high school leavers, the best work and education evidence available. Link: https://mn.gov/deed/newscenter/publications/trends/march-2026/somali.jsp
- Compass disability figures: 7.4 percent, give or take 1.5. Link: https://www.mncompass.org/topics/demographics/cultural-communities/somali

## Checked and found supported

- MN-ADDM 2022 data: 1 in 12 (8.26 percent), 1 in 28 overall, parts of Anoka, Hennepin and Ramsey counties (https://addm.umn.edu/detailed-findings/press-briefing-2025-09-26).
- Minneapolis study: 1 in 32 and 1 in 36, not significant; 1 in 62 and 1 in 80; all Somali children identified had intellectual disability (https://www.mprnews.org/story/2013/12/16/study-mpls-somali-white-children-equally-susceptible-to-autism).
- MDH 2014 study: 33 informants, 70 parents, 38 Somali; over four children on average; 19 percent single-parent; about a quarter said generalizing was hard (https://www.leg.mn.gov/docs/2014/mandated/140404.pdf).
- 1 in 29 for 2018 and 1 in 16 for 2020 (https://sahanjournal.com/health/autism-diagnosis-signs-symptoms-help-minnesota-somali-parents/ ; https://sahanjournal.com/immigration/maangaar-summit-somali-autism-minnesota/).
- *Maangaar* and *maangooni* and the older terms (https://sahanjournal.com/health/autism-somali-new-terminology-community-acceptance-minnesota/).
- Samatar's disputed birth year and ADC founding date (https://www.mnhs.org/mnopedia/search/index/person/samatar-hussein-1964-2013).
- Somali Museum 2011 and 2013 dates, address, hours and 1,500-plus artifacts (https://www.somalimuseum.org/mission-and-history). Copyright says 2025; hours may need a phone check.
- Riverside Plaza about 4,400 residents, majority East African descent, largest affordable housing claim (https://www.nps.gov/articles/riverside-plaza-mn.htm).
- Nadia Mohamed: 58 to 41 percent, "believed to be the first," and Atlantic Council fellowship (https://www.pbs.org/newshour/politics/minnesota-city-is-believed-to-be-the-first-in-the-u-s-to-elect-a-somali-american-as-mayor ; https://www.atlanticcouncil.org/expert/nadia-mohamed/).
- Somali Night April 3, 2026 and 28 years (https://www.northrop.umn.edu/events/somali-night-2026). The Somali Brief launch, September 2026 (https://tcbmag.com/sahan-journal-founder-mukhtar-ibrahim-launches-somali-american-media-company/). Warsame and the NAACP in June 2026 (https://www.fox9.com/news/minneapolis-naacp-demands-housing-chiefs-resignation-over-shameful-heritage-park-conditions).
