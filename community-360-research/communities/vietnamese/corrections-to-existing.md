# Vietnamese Minnesota page: corrections and checks

Page checked: https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/vietnamese
Date checked: October 30, 2026, except items marked
## Outdated figures

1. **Population and profile figures use the 2019 to 2023 Compass numbers.**
   - Page: about 33,891 people; 76.3 percent Twin Cities and 23.7 percent Greater Minnesota; 27.2 percent 17 or younger; 51.0 percent foreign-born; 68.6 percent other language at home; 37.3 percent English less than "very well"; 79.6 percent high school or more; 36.3 percent bachelor's or more; median household income about $90,000; poverty 9.1 percent; owner-occupied 69.5 percent; household size 3.01.
   - Compass now (2020 to 2024): 32,841 (give or take 3,477); 76.8 and 23.2 percent; 27.4 percent; 48.5 percent; 66.9 percent; 35.5 percent; 77.0 percent; 36.1 percent; $92,644 (give or take $9,598), in 2024 dollars; 10.5 percent; 70.1 percent; 2.95. The 65-and-older share is unchanged at 8.4 percent.
   - The page's dating caution is right, but the figures should be replaced and margins shown. All these changes are within the margins of error, so none shows a real shift by itself.
   - Link: https://www.mncompass.org/topics/demographics/cultural-communities/vietnamese

2. **The page has no Minnesota disability figure.** Compass shows 8.4 percent (give or take 1.8) with a disability and 6.0 percent (give or take 1.5) under 65. The State Demographic Center counts about 1,000 (give or take 500) adults ages 18 to 64. The disability section relies mostly on Orange County, California, reporting and national writers; Minnesota evidence should lead.
   - Links: https://www.mncompass.org/topics/demographics/cultural-communities/vietnamese ; https://mn.gov/admin/assets/The%20Economic%20Status%20of%20Minnesotans%202026_tcm36-717691.pdf

## Wording to correct

3. **"University Avenue and Frogtown, Minneapolis/St. Paul."** Frogtown and the Little Mekong stretch of University Avenue are in St. Paul. Visit Saint Paul places Little Mekong in Frogtown, between Mackubin and Marion streets. The city wording should not suggest Frogtown is in Minneapolis.
   - Link: https://www.visitsaintpaul.com/things-to-do/neighborhoods/little-mekong/

4. **"Little Mekong" appears among Vietnamese places without context.** Its organizers describe an Asian business and cultural corridor, and Visit Saint Paul ties its formation to Hmong settlement. It should not read as a Vietnamese district alone.
   - Links: https://www.littlemekong.com/ ; https://www.visitsaintpaul.com/things-to-do/neighborhoods/little-mekong/

5. **St. Adalbert start date.** Page: began in 1990 with Father Tim Kernan sponsoring two families. MNopedia itself disagrees: its text says the families moved in 1989, and its chronology says December 1990. The page should say "about 1990."
   - Link: https://www.mnhs.org/mnopedia/search/index/thing/origins-vietnamese-community-st-adalbert-church-st-paul

6. **Leaves out major recent facts.** VCM's 2026 Tết (Jan. 24 and 25, Burnsville Center), the sale of VCM's office in 2024, its move to Brooklyn Park, the Vietnamese Minnesotan Museum's 2025 founding and $200,000 goal, and the deportation reporting of 2025 and 2026 are not covered, or not fully.
   - Links: https://vietnam-minnesota.org ; https://www.mnhs.org/mnopedia/search/index/vietnamese-community-of-minnesota ; https://sahanjournal.com/arts-culture/vietnamese-minnesotan-museum-pop-up-exhibits/ ; https://asianews.network/?p=303930

## Checked and supported

- Phạm Văn Vy founding VCM in 1981 and serving as first president: MNopedia supports it.
- VCM over 20,000 attendees at the 2024 Lunar New Year: MNopedia supports it; the dates, Jan. 27 and 28, 2024, match VCM's list and FOX 9.
- Tri Tang elected president, August 2023: MNopedia supports it; VCM's leadership page lists him as president.
- More than 33,000 by 2023 (MNopedia) and more than 30,000 (VCM): both as the page states. MNopedia's own source for its number is World Population Review and Compass.
- St. Joseph Hiển once holding the only Vietnamese-language Mass in Minnesota: MNopedia supports it.
- About 200 Vietnamese families at St. Adalbert by 1996; Father Minh Vu first Vietnamese pastor in 2001: MNopedia supports them.
- Chùa Phật Ân at 475 Minnesota Avenue, Roseville, and MNopedia's description of it as the first Vietnamese temple: the temple's site, Candid and MNopedia support them. The temple's own site also uses "Saint Paul" for the same street address on one post.
- Vietnamese Social Services: 12,000-plus participants a year, about half Vietnamese and about 40 percent Karen, Elder Circle, and a Minneapolis Parks Foundation grant: VSS's site supports them.
- Christina Nguyen's 2024 James Beard Award for Best Chef: Midwest, for Hai Hai: MPR News and Hai Hai support it.
- St. Anne–St. Joseph Hiển's address and Vietnamese Masses: the archdiocese supports them.
- St. Adalbert's address: the archdiocese supports it.

## Could not support from pages reached

- Church of Saint Columba's Sunday 10:30 a.m. Vietnamese Mass.
- Judge Viet-Hanh Winchell's description as Minnesota's first Vietnamese American state court judge (Minnesota courts pages not reached). Her appointment by Gov. Walz to the Tenth Judicial District, chambered in Stillwater, is supported by Mitchell Hamline (https://mitchellhamline.edu/alumni/class-note/hon-viet-hanh-winchell-08/).
- Hoang Murphy's Foster Advocates history, Bush Fellowship and House run. A 2018 WCCO story confirms only that he spoke as the son of Vietnamese immigrants from St. Paul's East Side.
- Bao Phi's biography and awards.
- Asian American Press's status.
- Vietnamese Minnesotans Association: a summary of its IRS Form 990 filings supports a St. Paul nonprofit founded in 1993 that focuses on personal care assistance and homemaking for mostly Vietnamese American clients (https://minnesota.grantwatch.com/organization-profile/573694/vietnamese-minnesotans-association/). Any other details on the page about it were not confirmed.
- KFAI Vietnam MN Radio. Page: a 60-minute Vietnamese-language program whose latest posted show was dated April 26, 2026. KFAI's current weekly schedule lists no Vietnamese-language program (https://kfai.org/schedule/), and the program page (https://kfai.org/program/vietnammnradio/) returned a server error. Whether the program is still on the air should be confirmed with the station.
- VSS's history items: 1987 founding, house as collateral, Point of Light, Peace Prize, and street address.
- MNopedia's and Phật Ân's founding details for the Buddhist Association (May 15, 1976, and building dates). The Carleton College exhibit refused connections.
- DHS language lists, hepatitis B guidance, and Medical Assistance disability rules. These were not rechecked in this pass.
- National figures and organizations in "Beyond Minnesota" were not rechecked, as they are outside this community's Minnesota evidence.
