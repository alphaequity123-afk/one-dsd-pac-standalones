# Corrections to the existing Amharic-speaking Minnesota page

Page checked: https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/amharic
Date checked: October 31, 2026

## Outdated figures

1. **Claim:** "Minnesota Compass Ethiopian (ACS 2019–2023) is 40,324, with about 89.4 percent in the Twin Cities and 10.6 percent in Greater Minnesota. About 43.5 percent are age 17 or younger, and 61.7 percent are foreign-born. .. 74.9 percent speak a language other than English and 30.4 percent speak English less than 'very well'; median household income is about $57,106 (2022 dollars, averaged), poverty is 13.0 percent, and 65.3 percent are renters." The "Counts, Dated" section repeats these and adds 65+ 3.6%, bachelor's or higher 24.6%, household size 3.09, disability 5.4%.
   **What the opened source says now:** Compass has moved to 2020-2024 data in 2024 dollars: 45,159 people used in analysis (give or take 5,404), and a 2024 single-year total of 53,754 (give or take 12,826); Twin Cities 89.7%; Greater Minnesota 10.3%; 17 and younger 43.8%; 65 and older 3.5%; foreign-born 59.8%; language other than English 76.2%; English less than "very well" 30.2%; median household income $63,667; poverty 10.9%; renters 64.4%; average household size 3.30; disability 5.5%.
   **Link:** https://www.mncompass.org/topics/demographics/cultural-communities/ethiopian
   **Note:** The old figures were not wrong for their period, but they should be replaced. The statement that a household size of 3.09 is "smaller than some other Compass communities" should be dropped or rechecked, since the current figure is 3.30.

2. **Claim:** "Minnesota has no reliable count of Amharic speakers."
   **What opened sources say:** Correct for current years, but there is an official older count and a current count of students. The Census Bureau estimated 9,490 Amharic speakers age 5+ in Minnesota for 2009-2013 (give or take 1,009), seventh among states; since then its standard tables group Amharic with Somali and other Afro-Asiatic languages. Minnesota schools reported 2,379 students with Amharic as primary home language in 2024-25, 948 of them English learners.
   **Links:** https://www.census.gov/data/tables/2013/demo/2009-2013-lang-tables.html ; https://www.lrl.mn.gov/docs/2026/other/261381.pdf
   **Suggested change:** Add these, dated, so staff are not left with no number at all.

## Claims that could not be supported

3. **Claim:** "MSP Magazine records that Minnesota admitted 17 refugees from Ethiopia in 1982, the first African refugees in the state's telling of that file."
   **What the opened source says:** Mpls.St.Paul Magazine's 2024 "The New Ethiopian Scene" article contains no 1982 figure or claim about the first African refugees. No other source for this was found.
   **Link:** https://mspmag.com/eat-and-drink/the-new-ethiopian-scene/
   **Suggested change:** Remove or find the original source.

4. **Claim:** Partners include "Ethiopian Community Development Council affiliates," and the sources list "Ethiopian Community Development Council affiliates."
   **What the page itself says elsewhere:** Its own ECDC entry says ECDC's pages "do not list a Minnesota site." The two statements conflict. No Minnesota ECDC affiliate was found.
   **Suggested change:** Remove ECDC from the Minnesota partners list, or keep it only as a national organization.

5. **Claim:** "Amharic is on the CLAS fifteen interpreter cards."
   **Note:** This refers to an internal department resource that could not be checked from public sources. Not marked as an error.

6. **Claim:** KFAI "Voices of Ethiopia" airs "on Sundays from 4 to 5 p.m., with hosts Ahmed Wassie, Altish AsfawAmde and Sewitt Woldegiorgis."
   **What opened sources say:** The latest playlist that opened is from Sunday, April 17, 2022, 4 to 5 p.m., hosted by Ahmed Wassie. KFAI's own site returned a server error, so the current schedule and the other two hosts could not be confirmed.
   **Link:** https://spinitron.com/KFAI/pl/15852332/Voices-of-Ethiopia
   **Suggested change:** Mark the schedule as unconfirmed or recheck with KFAI.

## Claims that need nuance

7. **Claim:** "Do not assume Cedar-Riverside, because that hub is Somali" and "not Cedar-Riverside as the default."
   **What opened sources say:** Cedar-Riverside is a Somali hub, but it is also a long-standing Ethiopian commercial center. A dining guide updated October 2026 calls it "the heart of Ethiopian Minneapolis," where The Red Sea has operated since 1990, and MPR News profiled an Amharic-speaking grocer on Cedar Avenue in 1999. School data for 2024-25 show the most Amharic-speaking English learners in Dakota County.
   **Links:** https://mplswithlove.com/guides/best-ethiopian-east-african-food ; https://archive.mpr.org/stories/1999/11/03/enuyes-merkato-ethiopian-grocery-store ; https://www.lrl.mn.gov/docs/2026/other/261381.pdf
   **Suggested change:** Say that Cedar-Riverside should not be the only place staff think of, rather than implying it is not an Ethiopian place, and add the south-metro suburbs.

8. **Claim:** "Minneapolis parishes (Debre Selam Medhanealem Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church among them, in Seward / Minnehaha)."
   **What the opened source says:** Debre Selam Medhanealem moved in April 2026 from 4401 Minnehaha Ave. to a new church at 2601 30th Ave. S. in Seward. Sunday services run 7 to 11 a.m. in Ge'ez and Amharic with some English.
   **Link:** https://www.longfellownokomismessenger.com/stories/debre-selam-medhanealem-ethiopian-orthodox-tewahedo-churchfinds-new-home-in-seward,161305
   **Suggested change:** Give the current Seward address.

9. **Claim:** "The 2020–2022 war in Tigray is current structure."
   **What opened sources say:** Fighting resumed in Tigray in October 2026, and fighting between the federal army and Fano militias in the Amhara region has continued since July 2023. The page does not mention the Amhara region conflict, which is the one most directly tied to Amhara households.
   **Links:** https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/15/ethiopian-forces-expand-control-in-tigray-as-militia-claims-eritrean-losses ; https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/3/22/ethiopias-army-says-more-than-300-fano-fighters-killed-in-renewed-fighting
   **Suggested change:** Update to say fighting is ongoing in 2026, in both Tigray and Amhara regions, attributed to named outlets.

10. **Claim:** "Minnesota Secretary of State voter materials include Amharic."
    **What opened sources say:** Supported. Sahan Journal (Sept. 22, 2025) reports the state's voter guides cover top languages including Amharic. Worth adding: polling places statewide post instructions only in Spanish, Hmong and Somali, and only one of 291 designated language minority census tracts lists Amharic, jointly as "Amharic/Oromo, Somali" (Tract 1034, Minneapolis Ward 5).
    **Links:** https://sahanjournal.com/immigration/minnesota-non-english-languages-census-data/ ; https://www.sos.mn.gov/media/eztgjxiz/2026voterlanguageaccess.pdf ; https://www.sos.mn.gov/media/5634/language-minority-districts-tracts-precincts-languages.xlsx

11. **Claim:** "The Multicultural Autism Action Network in Minnesota lists Amharic-language videos."
    **What the opened source says:** The Amharic page lists one video, on elopement (wandering) in autistic children. The site carries a 2024 copyright.
    **Link:** https://www.maanmn.org/ahmaric-language-videos
    **Suggested change:** Say "one Amharic video" so staff know how little there is.

## Claims checked and supported

- Ethiopian Community in Minnesota hours (Monday 9 to 11 a.m., Friday 3 to 5 p.m.) and 616 Rice St. address: supported by its contact page, https://ethiopiancommunitymn.org/contact/.
- Ethiopian Evangelical Church in Minnesota in Oakdale with Amharic Sunday, Wednesday and Saturday young adult services and an English youth service: supported, https://eecmn.org/.
- Getatchew Haile's arrival at HMML in Collegeville, cataloging of several thousand manuscripts by 1993 and MacArthur Fellowship: supported, https://hmml.org/stories/getatchew-haile-ethiopic-scholar/.
- Henok Degfu's 2005 departure after 12 defamation cases, Benghazi jail, 2007 UNHCR petition, 2008 founding of ZeHabesha as a 24-page English and Amharic monthly, studio off Snelling Avenue: supported, https://sahanjournal.com/profiles/ethiopian-journalist-zehabesha/.
- MPR News report of the 2005 State Demographer estimate of about 7,500 Ethiopian immigrants, with immigrant groups saying about double: supported, https://archive.mpr.org/stories/2005/05/19/ethiopians-diverse-group.
- Worthington congregation coverage in 2020 and 2022 under two names: supported; the 2022 story says the church was first established in Worthington in 2010 and received a permit to move into The Globe's office building at 300 11th St.
- MPR News, June 30, 2026, on the soccer tournament nearly not coming to Minnesota because of Operation Metro Surge: supported.
- Fasting: Mpls.St.Paul Magazine (2024) reports about 180 fasting days a year for observant Tewahedo Christians over 7 and 250 for clergy, consistent with the page's general statements.
