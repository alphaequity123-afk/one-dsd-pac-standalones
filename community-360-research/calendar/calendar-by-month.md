## January 2026

- Thursday, January 1: **Anniversary of the 1959 Revolution**. January 1, 1959: for some Cuban families a day of loss and exile, for others a day of history. [Cuban Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/cuban)
- Thursday, January 1: **Haitian Independence Day**. January 1 marks Haiti's 1804 independence, celebrated with soup joumou; Haiti was the first Black republic. [French-speaking Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/french)
- Thursday, January 1: **Karen New Year gathering in St. Paul**. Minnesota's public Karen New Year gathering in St. Paul, on the dates its organizers announce. [Karen Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/karen)
- Thursday, January 1: **New Year (Novy God)**. The big secular winter holiday of Soviet times, with a decorated tree and Grandfather Frost; still central for some families. [Russian-speaking Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/russian-speaking), [Ukrainian Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/ukrainian)
- Thursday, January 1: **New Year's Day**. January 1, the first legal holiday of Minnesota's civic year; state and many local offices close. [American culture](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/american)
- Thursday, January 1: **Shōgatsu (Japanese New Year)**. Japan's New Year on January 1; in Minnesota, marked in homes and at the Japan America Society's Shinnenkai party. [Japanese Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/japanese)
- Friday, January 2: **Ancestors' Day (Haiti)**. January 2, the day after Haitian Independence Day, honors the founders of Haiti and those who died for independence. [French-speaking Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/french)
- Tuesday, January 6: **Epiphany (Día de los Reyes Magos)**. The Magi's visit, Jan. 6; Colombia's public holiday moves to the following Monday. [Colombian Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/colombian), [Cuban Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/cuban), [Ecuadorian Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/ecuadorian), [Guatemalan Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/guatemalan), [Latino Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/latino), [Mexican Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/mexican), [Puerto Rican Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/puerto-rican)
- about Tuesday, January 6: **Epiphany and Galette des Rois**. Epiphany in early January, when French families share a galette des rois with a hidden charm; Twin Cities patisseries bake them. [French-speaking Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/french)
- Wednesday, January 7: **Genna**. Ethiopian Orthodox Christmas, usually January 7, after the Advent fast; services in Ge'ez and Amharic. [Amharic-speaking Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/amharic), [Ethiopian Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/ethiopian)
- Wednesday, January 7: **Orthodox Christmas (Božić)**. Christmas for Serbian Orthodox Christians, including Bosnian Serbs, on January 7, with Christmas Eve on January 6. [Bosnian Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/bosnian), [Russian-speaking Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/russian-speaking), [Ukrainian Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/ukrainian)
- Monday, January 12: **Haiti earthquake remembrance**. Anniversary of the January 12, 2010 magnitude 7.0 earthquake near Léogâne, Haiti; for many families, the start of TPS. [French-speaking Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/french)
- Tuesday, January 13: **Korean American Day**. Jan. 13 marks the anniversary of Korean immigration to the United States; no Minnesota observance was found. [Korean Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/korean)
- Thursday, January 15: **Feast of the Black Christ of Esquipulas**. Feast of Guatemala's most venerated crucifix; Worthington parishioners celebrate with Mass, marimba, dance and a procession. [Guatemalan Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/guatemalan)
- Friday, January 16: **Salvadoran Peace Accords anniversary**. January 16 recalls the 1992 accords that ended El Salvador's civil war; since 2022 El Salvador calls it a day for war victims. [Salvadoran Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/salvadoran)
- Saturday, January 17 to January 18: **CAAM Chinese Dance Theater Lunar New Year Production**. Annual Lunar New Year dance-drama by the Chinese American Association of Minnesota's Chinese Dance Theater in St. Paul. [Chinese Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/chinese)
- about Sunday, January 18: **Feast of the Santo Niño (Sinulog)**. Visayan feast of the child Jesus on the third Sunday of January, with Mass and Sinulog dancing. [Filipino Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/filipino)
- Monday, January 19: **Martin Luther King Jr. Day**. Federal holiday for Dr. King's life and work, marked with marches and elder gatherings in Minnesota. [African American Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/african-american), [American culture](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/american), [Minnesota culture](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/minnesota)
- Monday, January 19: **Timkat**. Orthodox Epiphany, marking the baptism of Jesus, usually January 19; a major church gathering. [Amharic-speaking Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/amharic), [Ethiopian Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/ethiopian)
- Saturday, January 24: **Thamizhar Thiruvizha (TCTA Pongal festival)**. Twin Cities Tamil Association's January arts festival, held to celebrate Pongal. [Indian (Asian Indian) Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/indian)
- Saturday, January 24 to January 25: **Vietnamese Community of Minnesota Tết Festival**. The community's large public Tết fair, hosted since 1981, with performances, dragon dances and vendors. [Vietnamese Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/vietnamese)
- about Saturday, January 24: **Vietnamese Minnesotan Museum Lunar New Year Celebration**. A young museum's Lunar New Year program; its site lists Jan. 24 without a year. [Vietnamese Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/vietnamese)
- Saturday, January 24: **Winter Frolic, Snake River Fur Post**. Annual MNHS winter day of fur-trade era games, curling stones, snowshoeing and voyageur reenactors near Pine City. [French-speaking Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/french)
- about Sunday, January 25: **Burns Night**. Scottish celebration of poet Robert Burns; Mapleton's Burns event dates to 1857 and reached its 143rd in 2019. [European American Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/european-american)
- Sunday, January 25: **Feast of San Pablo**. Families from Axochiapan, Morelos, honor their patron saint in Minneapolis with Masses, a street procession, dance and food. [Mexican Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/mexican)
- Monday, January 26: **Republic Day (India)**. Marks India's constitution taking effect in 1950. [Indian (Asian Indian) Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/indian)
- Saturday, January 31: **Karen Revolution Day**. Marks the start of the Karen uprising on January 31, 1949. [Karen Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/karen)

## February 2026

- Sunday, February 1: **Anniversary of the 2021 Myanmar military coup**. February 1, 2021 coup anniversary; Minnesota's Burma Caucus co-chair launched the caucus on the 2024 anniversary. [Burmese Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/burmese)
- Sunday, February 1: **Black History Month (begins)**. A month of Black history programs across Twin Cities museums, theaters, libraries and community centers. [African American Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/african-american), [American culture](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/american)
- about Monday, February 9: **Feast of St. Maron**. February 9 honors St. Maron, patron of the Maronite Church; a public holiday in Lebanon and St. Maron parish's feast. [Lebanese Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/lebanese)
- Wednesday, February 11: **Karen National Day**. Recalls a February 11, 1948 demonstration in which Karen people called for their own homeland. [Karen Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/karen)
- Thursday, February 12: **Union Day (Myanmar)**. February 12 marks the 1947 Panglong Agreement between Aung San and ethnic leaders; no Minnesota observance was found. [Burmese Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/burmese)
- Sunday, February 15: **Chinese Community Center Lunar New Year Festival**. Large Lunar New Year street-fair style festival at Asia Mall in Eden Prairie with performances, food and market stalls. [Chinese Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/chinese)
- Monday, February 16 to April 12: **Abiy Tsom (Great Lent)**. Orthodox fast of 55 days before Fasika, without meat, eggs or dairy. [Ethiopian Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/ethiopian)
- Monday, February 16 to February 17: **Carnaval**. The Monday and Tuesday before Lent, a national holiday in Ecuador; Ecuadorian students in Minneapolis have celebrated with foam and glitter. [Ecuadorian Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/ecuadorian)
- Monday, February 16 to February 19: **Giao Thừa and Tết at Chùa Phật Ân**. A Roseville temple's year-end gathering, New Year's Eve service with lion dance, and first three days of Tết. [Vietnamese Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/vietnamese)
- about Monday, February 16: **Maronite Ash Monday (start of Lent)**. Maronite Catholics begin Lent on the Monday before the Western Ash Wednesday. [Lebanese Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/lebanese)
- about Monday, February 16 to February 22: **Maslenitsa (Butter Week)**. A pancake-filled farewell to winter in the week before Orthodox Lent; TMORA held a blini and folk-song evening in 2026. [Russian-speaking Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/russian-speaking)
- about Monday, February 16: **Washington's and Lincoln's Birthday**. Third Monday in February; Minnesota law keeps the name Washington's and Lincoln's Birthday for what many call Presidents' Day. [American culture](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/american)
- Tuesday, February 17: **Lunar New Year**. Start of the Chinese lunisolar year: family reunions, dumplings and fish, red envelopes, and lion dances across Minnesota. [Chinese Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/chinese)
- Tuesday, February 17: **Maslenitsa at TMORA (2026 event)**. Blini, tea and Russian village songs by the folk group Nitka at the Museum of Russian Art's Education Center. [Russian-speaking Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/russian-speaking)
- Tuesday, February 17: **Seollal**. Korean Lunar New Year: family rites, bowing to elders, tteokguk soup; Minnesota groups hold a public celebration each winter. [Korean Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/korean)
- Tuesday, February 17: **Tết Nguyên Đán**. The Vietnamese Lunar New Year, a time for family, temple and parish gatherings, and community fairs. [Vietnamese Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/vietnamese)
- Wednesday, February 18: **Ash Wednesday**. Start of Lent; many Catholics and some Lutherans receive ashes on the forehead. [European American Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/european-american), [Filipino Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/filipino), [Guatemalan Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/guatemalan), [Latino Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/latino), [Mexican Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/mexican), [Salvadoran Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/salvadoran)
- about Wednesday, February 18 to March 19: **Ramadan night prayers and community iftars**. Evening fast-breaking meals and night prayers hosted by mosques, churches, nonprofits and businesses during Ramadan. [Arabic-speaking Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/arabic-speaking)
- about Wednesday, February 18: **Start of Ramadan**. Month of daytime fasting, observed by African American Muslims and many other Minnesotans. [African American Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/african-american), [Arabic-speaking Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/arabic-speaking), [Bosnian Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/bosnian), [Ethiopian Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/ethiopian), [Indian (Asian Indian) Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/indian), [Kenyan Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/kenyan), [Lebanese Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/lebanese), [Nigerian Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/nigerian), [Oromo Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/oromo), [Somali Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/somali)
- Thursday, February 19: **Day of Remembrance (Executive Order 9066)**. February 19 marks the 1942 order that led to Japanese Americans' forced removal; the Twin Cities JACL holds an annual program. [Japanese Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/japanese)
- Friday, February 20: **Ukrainian Lenten Fish Fry**. Friday fish and pierogi dinners at the Ukrainian American Community Center through Lent, raising money for aid and newcomers. [Ukrainian Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/ukrainian)
- Saturday, February 21: **Seollal celebration in White Bear Lake (Korean Cultural Association and JangmiArts)**. [Korean Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/korean)
- Tuesday, February 24: **Anniversary of Russia's full-scale invasion**. February 24 marks the 2022 full-scale invasion; Minnesota's Ukrainian community holds commemorations, not celebrations. [Ukrainian Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/ukrainian)
- Tuesday, February 24: **Anniversary of Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine**. A painful date for many Russian-speaking households; marked in Minnesota with vigils and anti-war gatherings. [Russian-speaking Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/russian-speaking)
- Wednesday, February 25: **EDSA People Power anniversary**. Feb. 25 remembers the 1986 People Power uprising that ended the Marcos dictatorship. [Filipino Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/filipino)
- Wednesday, February 25: **Twin Cities Dumpling Feast**. Annual Lunar New Year dumpling-making dinner with performances at Peking Garden in St. Paul. [Chinese Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/chinese)

## March 2026

- Wednesday, March 4 to April 1: **Minnesota Cuban Film Festival**. Yearly series of films from Cuba at The Main in Minneapolis, with discussion after each screening, held since 2010. [Cuban Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/cuban)
- about Monday, March 9: **Nic Zapko Day**. Minnesota day declared March 9, 2021, honoring Deaf interpreter Nic Zapko's pandemic briefings in ASL. - about Wednesday, March 11: **Decoration Day (Liberia)**. Liberian national day for cleaning and decorating family graves; no Minnesota observance was found. [Liberian Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/liberian)

[European American Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/european-american)
- Tuesday, March 17: **St. Patrick's Day**. March 17; St. Paul's downtown parade, held since the 1960s, draws thousands of marchers, Irish and otherwise. [American culture](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/american), [European American Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/european-american)

- about Friday, March 20: **Eid al-Fitr**. End of the Ramadan fast, kept by African American Muslims and many other Minnesotans. [African American Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/african-american), [Arabic-speaking Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/arabic-speaking), [Bosnian Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/bosnian), [Ethiopian Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/ethiopian), [Indian (Asian Indian) Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/indian), [Kenyan Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/kenyan), [Lebanese Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/lebanese), [Liberian Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/liberian), [Nigerian Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/nigerian), [Oromo Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/oromo), [Somali Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/somali)
