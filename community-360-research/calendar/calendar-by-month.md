# Cultural calendar by month, 2026 to 2028

Dates below are calculated. Where an Islamic date depends on the moon, it is marked "about": the actual day is set by each mosque from moon sighting or its chosen calculation, and can be a day later or earlier. Celebrations whose dates are announced each year are listed at the end.

## Dates announced each year

- **Obon** (japanese): Mid-July or mid-August depending on the region and temple; local dates are announced by the organizers.
- **Loy Krathong** (thai): Full moon of the twelfth Thai lunar month, usually November.
- **Hmong New Year (Minnesota)** (hmong): Set each year by the organizing groups. Hmong New Year celebrations traditionally follow the harvest; in Minnesota they run from about October to December, and each city or organization picks its own dates and venue.
- **Irreecha** (oromo): Announced each year; held around the end of the Ethiopian rainy season, usually on a weekend in late September or early October.
- **Ramadan night prayers and community iftars** (somali, arabic-speaking, oromo, bosnian): Announced by each mosque and community group during Ramadan.
