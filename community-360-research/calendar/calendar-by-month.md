# Minnesota cultural calendar by month, 2026 to 2028

A plain date has an opened source stating it. "About" means the day is not yet confirmed for that year: moon-sighted dates are set by each mosque or religious authority, and other dates here are projections until a source confirms them.

[Vietnamese Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/vietnamese)

## January 2026

- Thursday, January 1: **New Year (Novy God)**. The big secular winter holiday of Soviet times, with a decorated tree and Grandfather Frost; still central for some families. - Tuesday, January 6: **Día de los Reyes**. The visit of the Magi; a major gift day in many Puerto Rican and Mexican families. - Wednesday, January 7: **Orthodox Christmas (Božić)**. Christmas for Serbian Orthodox Christians, including Bosnian Serbs, on January 7, with Christmas Eve on January 6. [Bosnian Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/bosnian), [Russian-speaking Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/russian-speaking), [Ukrainian Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/ukrainian)

[Amharic-speaking Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/amharic), [Ethiopian Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/ethiopian)

## February 2026

- Sunday, February 1: **Anniversary of the 2021 Myanmar military coup**. February 1, 2021 coup anniversary; Minnesota's Burma Caucus co-chair launched the caucus on the 2024 anniversary. [Burmese Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/burmese)

- Wednesday, February 11: **Karen National Day**. Recalls a February 11, 1948 demonstration in which Karen people called for their own homeland. [Karen Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/karen)
- Thursday, February 12: **Union Day (Myanmar)**. February 12 marks the 1947 Panglong Agreement between Aung San and ethnic leaders; no Minnesota observance was found. [Burmese Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/burmese)

- about Monday, February 16 to April 11: **Abiy Tsom (Great Lent)**. Orthodox fast of 55 days before Fasika, without meat, eggs or dairy. [Ethiopian Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/ethiopian)
- Monday, February 16 to February 19: **Giao Thừa and Tết at Chùa Phật Ân**. A Roseville temple's year-end gathering, New Year's Eve service with lion dance, and first three days of Tết. - about Monday, February 16: **Maronite Ash Monday (start of Lent)**. Maronite Catholics begin Lent on the Monday before the Western Ash Wednesday. [Lebanese Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/lebanese)
- about Monday, February 16 to February 22: **Maslenitsa (Butter Week)**. A pancake-filled farewell to winter in the week before Orthodox Lent; TMORA held a blini and folk-song evening in 2026. [Russian-speaking Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/russian-speaking)
- Tuesday, February 17: **Lunar New Year**. Start of the Chinese lunisolar year: family reunions, dumplings and fish, red envelopes, and lion dances across Minnesota. [Chinese Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/chinese)
- Tuesday, February 17: **Maslenitsa at TMORA (2026 event)**. Blini, tea and Russian village songs by the folk group Nitka at the Museum of Russian Art's Education Center. [Russian-speaking Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/russian-speaking)
- Tuesday, February 17: **Seollal**. Korean Lunar New Year: family rites, bowing to elders, tteokguk soup; Minnesota groups hold a public celebration each winter. [Korean Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/korean)
- Tuesday, February 17: **Tết Nguyên Đán**. The Vietnamese Lunar New Year, a time for family, temple and parish gatherings, and community fairs. [Vietnamese Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/vietnamese)
- Wednesday, February 18: **Ash Wednesday**. Start of Lent. [European American Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/european-american), [Filipino Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/filipino), [Guatemalan Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/guatemalan), [Latino Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/latino), [Mexican Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/mexican), [Salvadoran Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/salvadoran)

- Thursday, February 19: **Day of Remembrance (Executive Order 9066)**. February 19 marks the 1942 order that led to Japanese Americans' forced removal; the Twin Cities JACL holds an annual program. - Friday, February 20: **Ukrainian Lenten Fish Fry**. Friday fish and pierogi dinners at the Ukrainian American Community Center through Lent, raising money for aid and newcomers. [Ukrainian Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/ukrainian)

- Tuesday, February 24: **Anniversary of Russia's full-scale invasion**. February 24 marks the 2022 full-scale invasion; Minnesota's Ukrainian community holds commemorations, not celebrations. - Tuesday, February 24: **Anniversary of Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine**. A painful date for many Russian-speaking households; marked in Minnesota with vigils and anti-war gatherings. [Russian-speaking Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/russian-speaking)

## Dates each organizer announces

- **Hallie Q. Brown Day** (African American Minnesota): Around April 1; the center's page describes it in 2025.
- **Ramadan night prayers and community iftars** (Arabic-speaking Minnesota): Announced by each mosque and community group during Ramadan.
- **Ramadan night prayers and community iftars** (Bosnian Minnesota): Announced by each mosque during Ramadan.
- **Rochester Bosnian community soccer tournament** (Bosnian Minnesota): Held in the first week of July by its organizers; dates announced each year.
- **Kathina (Kathein) Robe Offering** (Burmese Minnesota): Held generally after the October full moon (Thadingyut) that ends the rains retreat, within the following month, on a date the temple chooses.
- **Sticky Rice Festival (Htamane)** (Burmese Minnesota): Traditionally at the full moon of the Myanmar month of Tabodwe; the Minnesota temple says generally in February, on a date it sets.
- **Karenni Kay Htoe Boe Festival** (Burmese Minnesota): An April weekend chosen each year by the Karenni Community of Minnesota.
- **Mon National Day** (Burmese Minnesota): Set by the traditional Mon calendar; no Minnesota date was found.
- **Ashenda** (Ethiopian Minnesota): At the end of the 15-day Filseta fast in August. Africanews reported it on Aug. 23, 2026 without an exact date, so none is stated here.
- **Dec. 13, 2003 Gambella killings** (Ethiopian Minnesota): Fixed date of the event. No organized annual Minnesota observance was found, so none is claimed and no calendar dates are given.
- **Pasko sa Nayon** (Filipino Minnesota): Set each year by the Cultural Society of Filipino Americans of Minnesota, in the Christmas season.
- **Paskong Pilipino** (Filipino Minnesota): Set each year by the Fil-Minnesotan Association.
- **Philippine Day (Minnesota)** (Filipino Minnesota): Set each year by the Cultural Society of Filipino Americans of Minnesota. The 2002 event was March 24.
- **Pamana** (Filipino Minnesota): Once every four years, set by the Cultural Society of Filipino Americans of Minnesota.
- **Pista sa Nayon (FilAmMinDak)** (Filipino Minnesota): An annual summer weekend set by FilAmMinDak.
- **Twin Ports and Brooklyn Park Hmong New Year gatherings** (Hmong Minnesota): Set locally each year. Brooklyn Park held its event on Dec. 13, 2025. The University of Minnesota Duluth Hmong Student Association held one in 2025 and planned to return.
- **Hindu Society of Minnesota Diwali** (Indian (Asian Indian) Minnesota): Set each year by the Hindu Society. 2026 date not found.
- **Vaisakhi** (Indian (Asian Indian) Minnesota): Traditionally mid-April; no page I opened states a date. Local gatherings are set by the gurdwara and Punjabi groups.
- **Pongal** (Indian (Asian Indian) Minnesota): Mid-January. Local date set by the group.
- **Bonalu** (Indian (Asian Indian) Minnesota): Set locally. 2026 date not found.
- **Ganesh Utsav** (Indian (Asian Indian) Minnesota): Set locally by the association.
- **Obon** (Japanese Minnesota): Mid-July or mid-August depending on region and temple in Japan. Minnesota dates are set by each organizer; see the separate Como Park Obon Festival entry.
- **Buddhist New Year / Water Festival** (Karen Minnesota): Mid-April, per KOM; exact date not stated on any opened page.
- **Novitiate Ceremony (Harris)** (Karen Minnesota): Announced by organizers; only the 2025 date was found. Whether it happens every year is not established.
- **Cambodian Heritage Day (Minnesota)** (Cambodian Minnesota): Set by the Cambodian American Partnership of Minnesota; held Saturday, Sept. 23, 2023 and Saturday, Sept. 27, 2025. Not necessarily annual.
- **Boun Pha Vet (King Vessantara festival)** (Lao Minnesota): Set by each organizer; no fixed Minnesota date was found.
- **Latine Day at the Capitol** (Latino Minnesota): Set each year by the Minnesota Council on Latino Affairs during session.
- **Irreecha (traditional thanksgiving)** (Oromo Minnesota): A traditional date at the end of the rainy season, usually September or October. A religious leader traditionally announces it by watching the sky and lunar calendar, Teferi Tafa told Ampers. Minnesota dates are set by organizers and are listed as separate entries.
- **Ramadan night prayers and community iftars** (Oromo Minnesota): Announced by each mosque and community group during Ramadan.
- **County fair season** (Rural and Greater Minnesota): Set by each county fair board, mostly July and August.
- **Fall harvest** (Rural and Greater Minnesota): Varies with crop and weather, roughly September through early November.
- **Judy Garland Fan Festival** (Rural and Greater Minnesota): Set by museum.
- **Ramadan night prayers and community iftars** (Somali Minnesota): Announced by each mosque or group during Ramadan.
- **Somali Week and Lake Street Somali Day Festival** (Somali Minnesota): Announced each year by People in Action, around July 1.
- **Maangaar Summit** (Somali Minnesota): First held Sept. 2, 2024; recurrence not confirmed.
- **Thai Picnic** (Thai Minnesota): Announced each year by the Thai Association of Minnesota; recent picnics fell on a late-August Sunday.
- **Ivana Kupala (midsummer)** (Ukrainian Minnesota): Midsummer; the Minnesota event date is set by its organizer. No dated Minnesota listing was found.
- **St. Anne–St. Joseph Hiển Summer Fair** (Vietnamese Minnesota): Announced each year by the parish.
