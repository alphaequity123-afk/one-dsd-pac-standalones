# Minnesota cultural calendar by month, 2026 to 2028

A plain date has an opened source stating it. "About" means the day is not yet confirmed for that year: moon-sighted dates are set by each mosque or religious authority, and other dates here are projections until a source confirms them.

[Vietnamese Minnesota](https://one-dsd-people-access-culture-production.up.railway.app/minnesota-communities/vietnamese)

## Dates each organizer announces

- **Hallie Q. Brown Day** (African American Minnesota): Around April 1; the center's page describes it in 2025.
- **Ramadan night prayers and community iftars** (Arabic-speaking Minnesota): Announced by each mosque and community group during Ramadan.
- **Ramadan night prayers and community iftars** (Bosnian Minnesota): Announced by each mosque and community group during Ramadan.
- **Ashenda** (Ethiopian Minnesota): At the end of the 15-day Filseta fast in August. Africanews reported it on Aug. 23, 2026 without an exact date, so none is stated here.
- **Dec. 13, 2003 Gambella killings** (Ethiopian Minnesota): Fixed date of the event. No organized annual Minnesota observance was found, so none is claimed and no calendar dates are given.
- **Twin Ports and Brooklyn Park Hmong New Year gatherings** (Hmong Minnesota): Set locally each year. Brooklyn Park held its event on Dec. 13, 2025. The University of Minnesota Duluth Hmong Student Association held one in 2025 and planned to return.
- **Hindu Society of Minnesota Diwali** (Indian (Asian Indian) Minnesota): Set each year by the Hindu Society. 2026 date not found.
- **Vaisakhi** (Indian (Asian Indian) Minnesota): Mid-April. Local gatherings are set by the gurdwara and Punjabi groups.
- **Pongal** (Indian (Asian Indian) Minnesota): Mid-January. Local date set by the group.
- **Bonalu** (Indian (Asian Indian) Minnesota): Set locally. 2026 date not found.
- **Ganesh Utsav** (Indian (Asian Indian) Minnesota): Set locally by the association.
- **Obon** (Japanese Minnesota): Mid-July or mid-August depending on the region and temple; local dates are announced by the organizers.
- **Buddhist New Year / Water Festival** (Karen Minnesota): Mid-April, per KOM; exact date not stated on any opened page.
- **Novitiate Ceremony (Harris)** (Karen Minnesota): Announced by organizers; only the 2025 date was found. Whether it happens every year is not established.
- **Latine Day at the Capitol** (Latino Minnesota): Set each year by the Minnesota Council on Latino Affairs during session.
- **Irreecha (traditional thanksgiving)** (Oromo Minnesota): A traditional date at the end of the rainy season, usually September or October. A religious leader traditionally announces it by watching the sky and lunar calendar, Teferi Tafa told Ampers. Minnesota dates are set by organizers and are listed as separate entries.
- **Ramadan night prayers and community iftars** (Oromo Minnesota): Announced by each mosque and community group during Ramadan.
- **Ramadan night prayers and community iftars** (Somali Minnesota): Announced by each mosque or group during Ramadan.
- **Somali Week and Lake Street Somali Day Festival** (Somali Minnesota): Announced each year by People in Action, around July 1.
- **Maangaar Summit** (Somali Minnesota): First held Sept. 2, 2024; recurrence not confirmed.
- **St. Anne–St. Joseph Hiển Summer Fair** (Vietnamese Minnesota): Announced each year by the parish.
