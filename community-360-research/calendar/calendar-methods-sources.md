# Calendar methods: opened sources

How each calendar system decides its dates. Every link below was opened on October 30, 2026, and the note says only what the opened page supports. Where a page could not be opened or did not answer the question, that is stated.

## Hijri calendar and moon sighting

- [Fiqh Council of North America, Calendar](https://fiqhcouncil.org/calendar/). States that its calendar uses calculation rather than requiring a sighting: after conjunction anywhere on the globe, the sun-moon angle at local sunset must be at least 8 degrees and the moon at least 5 degrees above the horizon, or the month begins a day later. It lists Ramadan and Eid dates for 2026 to 2028, which remain calculated, not observed.
- [Morocco World News, Saudi Arabia to sight the Eid al-Fitr 2026 moon on Wednesday](https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2026/03/283024/shawwal-saudi-arabia-to-sight-eid-al-fitr-2026-moon-on-wednesday/). Reports that the Saudi Supreme Court asked its moon sighting committees to look for the Shawwal crescent, with Eid on the next day if seen and a day later if not.
- [Morocco World News, Muslims in US will start celebrating Ramadan 2026 tomorrow](https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2026/02/279178/muslims-in-us-will-start-celebrating-ramadan-2026-tomorrow/). Reports that the Fiqh Council of North America set February 18, 2026 for North America and that mosques in cities including Chicago announced the date by calculation.
- [Robert van Gent, Utrecht University, Umm al-Qura calendar rules](https://webspace.science.uu.nl/~gent0113/islam/ummalqura_rules.htm). Describes the rule in use since 1423 AH, applied on the 29th of the lunar month, and names King Abdulaziz City for Science and Technology as the body that determines the calendar.
- [Moonsighting.org.uk, Saudi dating system](https://www.moonsighting.org.uk/saudi-dating-system.html). Calls Umm al-Qura the formal and civil calendar of Saudi Arabia, lists how its rule changed, and says it may not match actual crescent visibility. This is an advocacy site for sighting.

### How Minnesota mosques decide

No opened source explains how Minnesota mosques decide. The pages that did open say only this:

- [Sahan Journal, Ramadan iftars, fundraisers and mosque events in Minnesota, 2025](https://sahanjournal.com/arts-culture/ramadan-iftar-fundraisers-mosque-events-minnesota-2025/). Says the start could fall on an evening depending on the sighting of the crescent moon, and calls the Eid date tentative. It does not say who decides.

## Chinese lunisolar calendar

- [Hong Kong Observatory, Gregorian-Lunar Calendar Conversion Table 2026](https://www.hko.gov.hk/en/gts/time/calendar/text/files/T2026e.txt), and the [2027](https://www.hko.gov.hk/en/gts/time/calendar/text/files/T2027e.txt) and [2028](https://www.hko.gov.hk/en/gts/time/calendar/text/files/T2028e.txt) tables. Give the first day of each lunar month and each solar term day by day.
- [Hong Kong Observatory, The Chinese Agricultural Calendar Explained](https://www.hko.gov.hk/en/education/astronomy-and-time/time-service/00413-the-chinese-agricultural-calendar-explained.html). Months start at the new moon, and a leap month keeps twelve short lunar months in step with the year, seven leap months in nineteen years.
- [Hong Kong Observatory, What year is it today?](https://www.hko.gov.hk/en/education/astronomy-and-time/time-service/00506-what-year-is-it-today.html). Defines Lunar New Year as the second new moon after the Winter Solstice, not counting a leap month, and defines the 24 solar terms from the Earth's revolution around the Sun.
- [Korea Astronomy and Space Science Institute, Calendar data for 2028](https://astro.kasi.re.kr/life/post/calendarData?year=2028). Shows why Korean dates can differ by a day: lunar 1/1 is January 27, 2028 in Korea and January 26 in the Hong Kong table. The page says it is not the official almanac, which is the 월력요항.

## Hindu panchang

- [Positional Astronomy Centre, India Meteorological Department, History](https://packolkata.imd.gov.in/history.php). Traces the Rashtriya Panchang to the 1952 Calendar Reform Committee, and says the Department took over the work in 1955 with a solar calendar for civil use and a luni-solar system for religious use.
- [South African Hindu Maha Sabha, Calculating Hindu religious and festival dates](https://sahms.org.za/2022/10/28/calculating-hindu-religious-and-festival-dates/). A tithi is the time for the moon to gain 12 degrees on the sun, a festival is normally kept when its tithi prevails at sunrise, and dates depend on location.
- [Government of India DoP&T holiday list for 2027, as reproduced by StaffNews](https://www.staffnews.in/2026/07/list-of-holidays-for-the-year-2027-gazetted.html), and [Drik Panchang, Holi 2027 for New Delhi](https://www.drikpanchang.com/festivals/holi/festivals-holi-rangwali.html?year=2027). Together they show gazetted and almanac dates can differ: Holi is March 23, 2027 on the government list and March 22 on Drik Panchang.

## Theravada Buddhist calendar

- [Bank of Thailand, Notification No. 37/2569, holidays for 2027](https://www.bot.or.th/content/dam/bot/fipcs/documents/FPG/2569/EngPDF/25690175.pdf). Shows the Thai government sets Buddhist holy days year by year: Makha Bucha February 21, Visakha Bucha May 20, Asarnha Bucha July 18.
- [Fah Thai Magazine, Visakha Bucha Day](https://fahthaimag.com/visakha-bucha-day/). Says the day falls on the full moon of the sixth lunar month. This is a travel magazine, not a religious authority.
- [Myanmar Department of Civil Aviation, Public Holidays for Year 2026](https://ais.gov.mm/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/AIC-03-2026.pdf). Lists Myanmar's dates by lunar name. Full Moon Day of Kasong is April 30, 2026, a month before Thailand's Visakha Bucha on May 31, while Full Moon Day of Warso is July 29, the same day as Thailand's Asarnha Bucha. National Buddhist calendars can therefore part ways in a given year.
- [Andersen in Cambodia, 2026 public holiday schedule, Sub-Decree No. 167](https://kh.andersen.com/publications/cambodia-sets-official-2026-public-holiday-schedule/), and [Cambodianess, 2027 sub-decree](https://cambodianess.com/article/cambodia-has-22-public-holidays-in-2027). Show Cambodia sets Khmer New Year and Pchum Ben by yearly sub-decree. The 2027 article gives Pchum Ben as September 29 to October 1 yet also calls it an October holiday.

No Thai government or Sangha page explaining the calculation itself could be opened. A Journal of Southeast Asian Studies article on intercalation appeared in search but returned a 503 error.

## Ethiopian calendar

- [Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church, Calendar](https://www.ethiopianorthodox.org/english/calendar.html). Twelve months of 30 days and Pagumen of five days, six in the year of Luke, which precedes the Western leap year. The church dates years from the Incarnation, 7 or 8 years behind the Western system.
- [Embassy of Ethiopia, Ethiopian Time](https://ethiopianembassy.org/ethiopian-time/). Gives the month table with Meskerem starting September 11 and describes the calendar as seven to eight years behind the Gregorian.
- [Royal Norwegian Embassy in Addis Ababa, Closing Days 2026](https://www.norway.no/en/ethiopia/norway-ethiopia/about-embassy/2026/). Shows the fixed-rule dates in practice for 2026: Christmas January 7, Epiphany January 19, Ethiopian Good Friday April 10, Ethiopian New Year September 11.

## Julian calendar and the Orthodox churches

- [Ukrainian Orthodox Church of Canada, Dating Pascha in the Orthodox Church](https://www.uocc.ca/articles/dating-pascha-in-the-orthodox-church/). The Church bases Pascha on the Julian calendar, keeps the equinox at March 21, now 13 days off, and so Pascha cannot fall before April 3 on the Gregorian calendar.
- [American Carpatho-Russian Orthodox Diocese, Listing of Movable Feasts](https://www.acrod.org/prayercorner/movablefeasts/). Lists Pascha April 12, 2026, May 2, 2027 and April 16, 2028.
- [Russian Federation Labour Code, Article 112, via ConsultantPlus](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_34683/98ef2900507766e70ff29c0b9d8e2353ea80a1cf/). Names January 7 as Christmas and May 9 as Victory Day.

### Orthodox Church of Ukraine, 2023 change

- [Orthodox Times, Orthodox Church of Ukraine changes calendar](https://orthodoxtimes.com/orthodox-church-of-ukraine-changes-calendar/). The Church adopted the Revised Julian calendar for fixed feasts from September 1, 2023, moving Christmas to December 25 with Pascha unchanged, and authorized parishes and monasteries to adopt it. The Church's own decree was not opened.
- [Ukrainska Pravda, Zelenskyy signs law recognising 8 May](https://pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2023/06/12/7406493/). A related civil change: Ukraine marks the victory over Nazism on May 8, while May 9 remains the Russian date.

## Hebrew calendar

- [JNS, The intricacies of the Hebrew calendar leap year](https://www.jns.org/the-intricacies-of-the-hebrew-calendar-leap-year/). The months follow the moon but must stay aligned with the seasons, so a second Adar is added seven times in nineteen years. The seed list has no Hebrew-calendar entries, so no date check rests on this. This page does not address when the day begins.

## Karen New Year

- [UNHCR data portal, First Naypyidaw Karen New Year Celebrations](https://data.unhcr.org/ar/news/15390). Most Karen celebrate on the first day of Pyatho, the 10th Burmese month; Pantanaw Township and some Irrawaddy villages use the first of Tabodwe, the 11th month. It gives December 29 for 2016.
- [Radio Free Asia, Myanmar's Karen community celebrates New Year's](https://www.rfa.org/english/myanmar/2024/12/30/myanmar-karen-new-year-celebration). Says the holiday rests on the lunar calendar and the end of the rice harvest, celebrated December 30, 2024.
- [CalendarLabs, Myanmar holidays 2027](https://www.calendarlabs.com/holidays/myanmar/2027). A commercial site, used because no official 2027 list exists yet. It shows the observance falling twice in 2027, January 8 and December 28.
