A voluntary course for Minnesota Department of Human Services staff

The Water We Swim In

Most training on culture asks us to learn about other people's cultures. This course asks a different question first: what does our own culture look like, and how does it shape the way we serve Minnesotans whose cultures differ from ours?

Six modules, about 20 minutes each. Take them in order or one at a time. Each one opens with something familiar, explains the research in plain language with every fact linked to its source, offers one practice scenario, and ends with private reflection and one habit to try.

  2. 2Minnesota nice, decodedUnderstand the warmth, the indirectness and the newcomer freeze of Minnesota nice, and how each can look to people from other cultures.Not finished
  3. 3Where it comes fromTrace mainstream Minnesota culture to its roots in settlement, faith, farm life, climate and civic tradition, so that its everyday habits can be seen as one inheritance among many.Not finished

  6. 6Seeing across differenceUse the course's frameworks as mirrors for your own habits, and choose practices from Minnesota's strengths to keep.Not finished

How this course works

  • Private by design. Your checkmarks and notes are saved only in your own browser. Nothing is collected or graded.
  • Examples are labeled. Practice scenarios are composites written for teaching. They are not real cases or real people.
  • Content notes. Modules 4 and 5 include a short note before passages that describe violence, deaths or abuse, so you can pause or skip ahead.
  • Frameworks are mirrors. The research describes averages and tendencies. It never tells you who any one person is.

If something here brings up a hard memory or feeling, you can stop at any time. State employees can reach the State of Minnesota Employee Assistance Program for free, confidential support; your human resources office can connect you.

The course is drawn from the full essay, which has every source, and from the Community 360 profiles of 39 Minnesota communities.
