One DHS People, Access and Culture Program

A resource for DHS staff, run by the program.

About this resource

It is not connected to DHS information technology, case, or personnel systems. Its guidance supports everyday work and does not replace policy, legal advice, formal processes, or decisions made by responsible DHS offices. Authority labels show what each item can and cannot establish.

Privacy and access

Please leave out case, medical, personnel, complaint, and identifying details anywhere in this program. Notes you write are saved only on this computer, so another person who uses it could see them.
